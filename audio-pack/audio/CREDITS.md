# Deadwood Reblooms Audio Pack

All audio in this directory is distributed under CC0 1.0. The sources were edited into short, seamless mono ambience loops for Deadwood Reblooms; the thunderstorm loop also mixes two quiet thunder strikes over rain.

## Scene ambience

| Mod file            | Source                                                                                                                                                                                              | Creator                             | Scene direction                      |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| `day.ogg`           | [AMB Morning Sounds (Perfect Loop)](https://opengameart.org/content/amb-morning-sounds-perfect-loop)                                                                                                | Kresiek The Furry                   | distant, restrained daytime exterior |
| `night.ogg`         | [Random Sounds Samples](https://opengameart.org/content/random-sounds-samples), `cricket ambienc276.ogg`                                                                                            | Augmentality (Brandon Morris)       | sparse nocturnal exterior            |
| `encounter.ogg`     | [Lust](https://opengameart.org/content/lust), [horror breathing](https://opengameart.org/content/horror-breathing), and [Squeaky Bed Action](https://freesound.org/people/Breviceps/sounds/450623/) | AdoTheLimey, primbal, and Breviceps | pressure, breath, and muted movement |
| `meteor-shower.ogg` | [Next to You](https://opengameart.org/content/next-to-you)                                                                                                                                          | Joth                                | distant shimmer and uneasy wonder    |
| `blood-moon.ogg`    | [Grief and Anger](https://opengameart.org/content/grief-and-anger), original full-length encode                                                                                                     | TinyWorlds                          | restrained grief turning dangerous   |
| `solar-eclipse.ogg` | [Unsolved Investigation](https://opengameart.org/content/unsolved-investigation)                                                                                                                    | isaiah658                           | disorientation and oppression        |

The encounter loop follows Breeze of Lewdity's layered, action-oriented sound direction without copying its audio files.

The compact ambience loops use 24 kHz mono OGG Vorbis. The original full-length blood moon track is retained unchanged; the nine audio files total about 3.4 MiB.
