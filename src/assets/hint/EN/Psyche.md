## Sanity and fear

**Sanity** affects how well you can think and concentrate. **Fear** reflects how frightened you are at the moment. Sanity ranges from −500 to 1000 and starts at 1000. Fear ranges from 0 to 1000 and starts at 0.

## Sanity and learning

| Sanity range       | State        | Learning gains |
| ------------------ | ------------ | -------------- |
| Below −350         | Confused     | 60% of vanilla |
| −350 to below −200 | Muddled      | 70%            |
| −200 to below 0    | Troubled     | 75%            |
| 0 to below 150     | Dazed        | 90%            |
| 150 to below 400   | Unsettled    | 100%           |
| 400 to below 600   | Settled      | 105%           |
| 600 to below 900   | Clear-headed | 110%           |
| 900 and above      | Focused      | 120%           |

The multiplier applies to progress in science, maths, English and history earned in class, through reading and similar activities. Full sanity reduces fear gained from a fright by 25%, while minimum sanity increases it by 25%.

A non-consensual encounter with people, beasts or plants that causes trauma leaves you shaken, with a small sanity loss and fear gain based on the actual trauma recorded, capped at 10 sanity and 150 base fear per encounter. Waking from a special nightmare costs 10 sanity. The Ivory Wraith, undead raids and curses can also affect sanity.

When investigating the cavern or harbour, you can pursue strange sights and sounds or examine them cautiously. Following them further causes more fear, but can reveal a dry route around the flooded crossing or a gesture the guard recognises. Breaking a curse or dealing with a raid can help you recover.

## Easing fear

Fear below 200 does not affect willpower. Above that, it gradually weakens willpower checks, down to 75% at maximum fear.

At 400 fear, some locations offer **Stop and steady your breathing**. It takes five minutes and removes 200 fear and a little stress. You must wait thirty minutes before using it again.

Sleep, rest and company can ease fear. After an undead ship raid, resting by the hatch removes 200 fear and talking to Zephyr removes 100. Each is available once per raid.

Calming tablets from the hospital pharmacy remove 200 fear, halved at higher tolerance. Another dose while the effect is active does not stack. Dr Harper's prescription in the medicine drawer removes 100 fear, while the asylum's prescription removes 200. DoLX anti-anxiety tablets remove 150. Watch for the medication's side effects as well.

## Restoring sanity

Routine activities share a **daily allowance of 30 base recovery points**. Recovery above 600 sanity is halved, up to a maximum of 1000 sanity. Fear relief does not use the allowance and still applies when it runs out.

| Activity                                         | Base sanity recovery | Fear removed |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------- | ------------ |
| One full hour of sleep                           | 1                    | 80           |
| An hour of prayer for salvation                  | 10                   | 100          |
| Twenty minutes of writing in the loft            | 5                    | 60           |
| Prayer or an offering at the shrine              | 5                    | 100          |
| Talking, tea, a walk or time alone with Tsukihan | 5                    | 100          |
| Giving thanks at the ema rack                    | 10                   | 150          |

Shrine prayer and offerings share one use per day. Time with Tsukihan grants the sanity and fear recovery listed above at 20 affection or more. Giving thanks grants recovery once per wish. Interrupted sleep grants no recovery for an unfinished hour, and nightmares can still frighten you.

With **Revelation: The Book of Return** installed, shared work, choir practice, temple fieldwork, returning the Ivory Wraith's relic and Lost Lamb dreams can also affect sanity or fear. Routine sanity recovery shares the allowance above.
