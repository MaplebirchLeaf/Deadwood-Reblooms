## Sanity and fear

**Sanity** affects how well you can think and concentrate. **Fear** reflects how frightened you are at the moment. Sanity ranges from −500 to 1000 and starts at 1000. Fear ranges from 0 to 1000 and starts at 0.

## Sanity and learning

| Sanity range     | State        | Learning gains |
| ---------------- | ------------ | -------------- |
| Below −200       | Confused     | 60% of vanilla |
| −200 to below 0  | Troubled     | 75%            |
| 0 to below 150   | Dazed        | 90%            |
| 150 to below 600 | Unsettled    | 100%           |
| 600 to below 900 | Clear-headed | 110%           |
| 900 and above    | Focused      | 120%           |

The multiplier applies to progress in science, maths, English and history earned in class, through reading and similar activities. It does not affect skill training, recipes or money. Full sanity reduces fear gained from a fright by 25%, while minimum sanity increases it by 25%.

The Ivory Wraith, undead raids and curses can affect sanity. When investigating the cavern or harbour, you can pursue strange sights and sounds, examine them cautiously or leave. The cavern's stranger readings cause more fear, but can reveal a dry route around the flooded crossing or a gesture the guard recognises. Ordinary readings still let you complete the investigation. Breaking a curse or dealing with a raid can help you recover. Ordinary injuries do not cause extra sanity loss, and treating trauma does not restore sanity.

## Easing fear

Fear below 200 does not affect willpower. Above that, it gradually weakens willpower checks, down to 75% at maximum fear. It does not remove willpower growth you have earned.

At 400 fear, some locations offer **Stop and steady your breathing**. It takes five minutes and removes 200 fear and a little stress. You must wait thirty minutes before using it again.

Sleep, rest and company can ease fear. After an undead ship raid, resting by the hatch removes 200 fear and talking to Zephyr removes 100. Each is available once per raid.

Calming tablets from the hospital pharmacy remove 200 fear, halved at higher tolerance. Another dose while the effect is active does not stack. Dr Harper's prescription in the medicine drawer removes 100 fear, while the asylum's prescription removes 200. DoLX anti-anxiety tablets remove 150. Medication eases fear without restoring sanity, and its other side effects remain.

## Restoring sanity

Routine activities share a **daily allowance of 30 base recovery points**. Recovery above 600 sanity is halved, up to a maximum of 1000 sanity. Fear relief does not use the allowance and still applies when it runs out.

| Activity                              | Base sanity recovery | Fear removed |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------- | ------------ |
| One full hour of sleep                | 1                    | 80           |
| An hour of prayer for salvation       | 10                   | 100          |
| Twenty minutes of writing in the loft | 5                    | 60           |
| Prayer or an offering at the shrine   | 5                    | 100          |
| Talking, tea or a walk with Tsukihan  | 5                    | 100          |
| Giving thanks at the ema rack         | 10                   | 150          |

Shrine prayer and offerings share one use per day. Giving more money does not restore extra sanity. Activities with Tsukihan require at least 20 affection. Giving thanks grants recovery once per wish. Interrupted sleep grants no recovery for an unfinished hour, and nightmares can still frighten you.

With **Revelation: The Book of Return** installed, shared work, choir practice, the temple watch, returning the Ivory Wraith's relic and Lost Lamb dreams can also affect sanity or fear. Routine sanity recovery shares the allowance above.
