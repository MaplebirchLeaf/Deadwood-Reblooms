### Staying at Kylar's manor

First complete the vanilla **initial overnight stay at Kylar's manor**, then become Kylar's lover. Ask in Kylar's room while Kylar is active and at the manor **after 18:00**, or while their schedule explicitly puts them in the manor bedroom. The invitation makes the room available as a place to rest.

| Place        | What you can do                                                      | Check                                                                               |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Kylar's room | Wardrobe, mirror, games, and bed entries in sleepwear or undressed   | Shared interactions require Kylar at the manor. Night waking has different dialogue |
| Kitchen      | Make one cup of tea per day. The scene changes with Kylar's presence | It does not repeat after today's cup. Tea remains available while Kylar is away     |
| Schoolbook   | Help Kylar sort damaged notes once a day                             | English skill 400 repairs the exercise. A prefect badge changes the question        |

The manor mirror connects to Vanilla Plus's strange-mirror route. If Kylar has moved into one of your properties, some shared-home interactions move to the new address. The old room remains, while vanilla schedule and story events take priority. Full safehouse choices stay locked until Kylar invites you.
