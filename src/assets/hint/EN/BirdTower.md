### Requirements

Enable the Bird Tower module. It adds growth, feeding, personality and hunting content for the vanilla Great Hawk and their chicks. The content takes place in the Bird Tower and on the moor. Earn the Great Hawk's trust, then hatch and raise young hawks to follow their development.

### Growth stages

Chicks pass through five stages by age:

| Stage        | Age        | Notes                                                          |
| ------------ | ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hatchling    | 0–13 days  | Newly hatched, no feathers                                     |
| Nestling     | 14–34 days | Downy feathers, still in the nest                              |
| Fledgling    | 35–64 days | Juvenile plumage, leaves the nest but cannot fly               |
| First flight | 65–89 days | Wings lose their down; begins learning to fly                  |
| First hunt   | 90+ days   | Full flight feathers, learns to hunt, moves to a separate nest |

When a stage advances, entering the tower or stepping onto the perch plays the moult, first flight and first hunt scenes in turn, with the Great Hawk personally teaching the chicks to fly and hunt. Past stages never roll back, and advancing several days preserves the prompts for each crossed stage.

### Daily interactions

On the children's page, each hawk shows an activity based on age, personality and what the Great Hawk is doing, including resting, preening, begging, exploring, practising flight, watching the weather and following a parent. Activities follow vanilla's refresh interval. A daily nest event can happen at most once per location each day, then returns to the page you came from.

### Feeding

Appetites grow with age. During the nestling stage one lurker feeds every chick; after fledging each hawk needs two meals a day, and once they can hunt alone they no longer need to be fed.

- You can feed a lurker to a chosen chick or split it evenly among the nest.
- Feeding accumulates a running total. Extra meals during early growth can increase a chick's size; existing size and feeding history both nudge the personality roll.
- If you never enter the tower that day, the Great Hawk feeds the chicks while uninjured and not pregnant. These meals still count toward the total.

### Personalities

At 65 days, each hawk is assigned one of four personalities, following the vanilla fox trait system:

| Personality | Tendency                                                                            |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Clumsy      | Clumsy and slow to learn, loves cuddling a parent, prefers junk when hunting        |
| Sympathy    | Sociable with other creatures, loves exploring, prefers other quarry                |
| Clever      | Learns to speak faster, more self-centred, collects shiny things, prefers valuables |
| Dominant    | Territorial, large, fierce in a fight, loves hunting and eating, prefers lurkers    |

Personality biases what the flock hunts and gives different reactions in daily interactions, growth events and the random events that fire while hunting with your hawks.

### Hunting together

Hawks that have learned to fly and hunt will head out on their own.

- **Hunting alone**: young hawks hunt the moor by themselves. Travelling deep into the moor, you may run into one and trigger one of three random events.
- **Joining you**: when you set out to hunt alone, a hawk may catch up and ask to come along. With your hawks in tow, the events and text differ from hunting with the Great Hawk, and the loot is divided up by personality at the end.
- **The new nest**: subadult hawks that have learned to hunt move into their own nest. A prompt appears in the tower, and once it is built you can visit the children's nest and keep upgrading it from the build menu.

The module also adds three feats: **Our First Flight**, **Wild Animals** and **Dances with Fox**.

### Nothing happens?

Check that the module is enabled and reloaded first. Growth events need the chick to reach the right age and the weather and the Great Hawk's state to match when you enter the tower or step onto the perch; if nothing fires, coming back later usually plays it. Feeding only takes lurkers stored at the tower, and when there are not enough you can split evenly or feed one at a time. The invitation to hunt together only appears at random when you hunt alone, at most once a day.
