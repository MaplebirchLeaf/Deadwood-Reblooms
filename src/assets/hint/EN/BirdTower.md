### Start at the hawk tower

Earn the Great Hawk's trust, then hatch and raise chicks at the tower. Bird Tower Expansion adds growth events, personalities, feeding and hunting together. Visit the tower or perch to see the next growth event.

| Age          | Stage                                               |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| 0–13 days    | Newly hatched, without feathers                     |
| 14–34 days   | Downy nestlings                                     |
| 35–64 days   | Able to leave the nest, but not yet fly             |
| 65–89 days   | Learning to fly                                     |
| From 90 days | Learning to hunt alone and preparing for a new nest |

### Care for your chicks

- Check their daily activities and hunger when viewing your children, then feed them lurkers brought back to the tower.
- While they remain nestlings, one lurker feeds all the children. After fledging, each chick needs two meals a day. Feed a particular chick or divide the food evenly, then return with more if needed.
- Extra meals during early growth can help increase their size. Once they hunt independently, they no longer need daily feeding from you.
- Moulting, first flight and first hunt follow their age. If the weather or the Great Hawk's situation prevents an event, return another day.

Food is consumed when you choose to feed the children. The result shows how many lurkers were used and the stock left after feeding. Reopening the result does not consume another meal; you can still choose to feed hungry children again.

### Personality and hunting

At 65 days, chicks gain a personality: **clumsy** chicks favour rubbish, **sympathetic** chicks favour other exploration, **clever** chicks favour valuables, and **dominant** chicks favour lurkers. Personality also changes daily dialogue and hunting reactions.

Once they can hunt, chicks venture onto the moor alone. You may meet them deep in the moor, or have a child ask to join you when you hunt alone. Watch for events on the journey and divide the loot when you return.

When the tower offers a new nest, build and improve it through the upgrade menu. You can keep visiting the children there.

### Feats

**Our First Flight**: witness the first flight. **Wild Animals**: drive off a wild animal with your chicks. **Dances with Fox**: witness a friendship between chick and fox.
