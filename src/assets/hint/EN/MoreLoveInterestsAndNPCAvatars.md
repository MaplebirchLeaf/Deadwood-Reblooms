### Choose your love interests

Choose people under **Character → Attitudes → Who do you consider a love interest?** Eligibility depends on each person's relationship progress.

| Awareness level shown on the character page | Love interests you can retain     |
| ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Level 3 or below                            | 1                                 |
| Level 4                                     | 2                                 |
| Level 5                                     | 3                                 |
| Level 6 or above                            | All currently eligible candidates |

After awareness changes, advance normally to the next day before checking the list. If you choose more than your awareness permits, later checks retain those at the front. Falling awareness can also remove later selections.

In New World, the **Polyamorous** trait lets you select several eligible partners without first raising awareness. New World uses the first six names for its love-interest order.

### Order and time together

- Click a selected person to remove them. Drag or use the arrows to reorder them. The first three occupy the original primary, secondary and tertiary love interest positions.
- The mod’s multiple-partner interactions also include partners placed fourth or later.
- With Orchard enabled, explicitly agreeing to a relationship with Remy also makes Remy eligible. See Orchard.
- Dates, overnight visits and living together still require the person's willingness, presence and individual conditions. See Finance for moving into your purchased home.
- Changing partners with the **Mate for Life** trait may affect your mental state; a warning appears before confirmation.

### Love locket

Wear a love locket to show it during ordinary conversations with Robin, Whitney, Kylar, Sydney, Avery, Alex, Gwylan, Remy or Tsukihan. You can do this once per person each day, with no extra time cost. The person must be present and able to talk. The option is unavailable during combat, forced encounters or overwhelming stress.

Reactions differ depending on whether the locket is empty or holds their photograph or someone else's. Explain your choice or close the locket. Change the photograph at a mirror.

Talk to Tsukihan on the veranda, during waking visits to the house, while tending the trees or while shopping in town. All locations share the same daily allowance. The option is absent while Tsukihan is asleep, bathing or when you have entered the house without an invitation.

Looking at a photograph in the mirror also reflects your stress, trauma, tiredness and some changes in the person's circumstances.

When you are close enough, have chosen the person as a love interest and carry their photograph in the locket, say you like keeping it with you to gain 1 affection and lose 2 stress. Remy requires a freely agreed relationship, not captivity or Stockholm syndrome. Reassuring Kylar can also slightly reduce jealousy.

### Social portraits

Social cards gain small portraits whose expressions and outfits follow the person's state. Larger scene portraits are controlled by NPC Sidebar Portrait.
