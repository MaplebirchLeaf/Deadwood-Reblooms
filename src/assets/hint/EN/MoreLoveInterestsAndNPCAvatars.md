### Prerequisites and entrance

Enable **MoreLoveInterestsAndNPCAvatars**, reload and open **Character → Attitudes → Love interests**. Candidate eligibility, awareness capacity and actual selection are separate requirements.

- **Eligibility** comes from each character's vanilla relationship conditions.
- **Capacity** comes from awareness, not love.
- **Selection** registers who the PC considers a love interest. Eligibility alone does not select someone. Dates and residency can check this selection.

### Vanilla awareness levels and capacity

Use the level and description shown under Awareness on the character page, rather than the internal romance eligibility numbering.

| Vanilla awareness level | Matching description                         | Retained love interests                            |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Three or below          | Innocence through basic sexual understanding | 1                                                  |
| Four                    | A deeper understanding of sex                | 2                                                  |
| Five                    | Having seen and knowing more than others     | 3                                                  |
| Six                     | Gazing into the abyss of depravity           | All currently eligible candidates, not merely four |
| Seven                   | Transcendent understanding                   | Same selection range as level six                  |

<span class="lewd">Reach the matching awareness level before retaining additional love interests.</span> Level six is enough for the expanded list. You need not wait for seven, and spare capacity does not unlock ineligible NPCs.

Vanilla refreshes romance awareness eligibility at day-change settlement. After advancing in an event, cross a game day normally and revisit Attitudes to check the list and prompt.

### Raising and losing awareness

This module has no slot purchase or dedicated awareness-training button. It uses vanilla changes. Some temple reading, confession branches, adult-shop work and other experiences raise awareness. Follow their actual stat prompts rather than expecting every reading or shift to give the same amount.

Awareness can fall too, such as confessing doubts at the temple and some asylum mirror stories. Purity, promiscuity, love and awareness are separate values. Raising another statistic does not satisfy the capacity table. High awareness also affects other vanilla states and stories, so check relevant traits and prompts.

### Adding, removing and ordering

1. Select a candidate to add them. Check capacity before adding more.
2. Select an already selected person to remove them. This removes the PC's selection without resetting love or past relationship stories.
3. Drag selected characters or use left/right arrows. The first three become vanilla primary, secondary and tertiary love interests.
4. Later selections participate in expanded love-interest checks. Stories reading only vanilla slots still depend on order. Put the desired primary person first.
5. Visit their actual location for dates or invitations. Each activity retains its own schedule, relationship, access and physical requirements.

<span class="red">Excess selections at low awareness cannot be retained reliably. Later checks keep the first eligible number and trim the rest.</span> Falling awareness and day-change updates can do this too. Put important selections first. The current interface does not simply grey out every candidate at capacity.

The list belongs to the current game save. Loading another save restores that save's list, unlike shared framework module toggles.

### Social avatar effects

Small avatars appear on existing vanilla Social cards. Expressions and layers can follow Robin's trauma and dominance, Kylar's love and jealousy, Sydney's purity and corruption, and other character states. These visuals do not raise love or unlock relationships.

Large scene portraits belong to **NPCSidebarPortrait**. A social avatar does not guarantee a scene portrait, or vice versa.
