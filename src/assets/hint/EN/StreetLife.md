## Where to start

Enable **Street Life** and reload. Street standing covers the town; the smuggler's pub is one base, not the whole street scene. Police crime records remain separate. Start with Landry at the Harvest Street pub, or meet Detective Grant at the police station.

| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Deliveries, recovery and fencing | Harvest Street pub → Landry                                 | Able to act, with no other cargo job active                                            |
| Weapons and ammunition           | Discovered smuggler's pub → weapon shop                     | Funds through your current merchant payment method; weapons use the handheld catalogue |
| Police bounties                  | Police station, 08:00–18:00                                 | An agreement with Grant and a criminal record that permits payment                     |

Check the **journal** for active jobs, cargo locations and next steps. **Statistics** shows actual payments, cargo losses and closed operations. Reading the journal does not reroll work or deliver goods and collect money for you.

## Farm-road races

Visit the first road section outside Harvest Street at night to ask about the races, watch a start or enter your own car. The route follows the road to the farmland, with novice and open classes. Ordinary cars can enter; motorcycles and bicycles do not compete in these classes. Snow cancels racing, while rain makes it easier to lose grip. Deal with any road encounter before approaching the gathering.

Check the entry fee, prizes and your vehicle before paying. Your car must actually be parked near the start; a car left in a garage cannot be summoned. Finish passenger and delivery work first. Driving skill, vehicle performance and decisions on the road all matter, so buying a more expensive car does not guarantee a win.

The novice entry fee is <span class="gold">£25</span>, with prizes of <span class="gold">£90 / £35 / £10</span> for the first three places. The open class requires 300 driving experience and costs <span class="gold">£100</span>, with prizes of <span class="gold">£300 / £150 / £50</span>. Entry takes two minutes, followed by twenty-one minutes for an uninterrupted run. Your car needs at least 45% condition and enough fuel for the whole course.

Choose whether to drive steadily, build speed or push harder at each stage. Steady driving is easier on the car; taking risks may gain places or cause a mistake. Traffic, bends and rain affect those choices. Each class allows one entry per night, including a run you withdraw from. A new racing night begins at 4 am; midnight does not reset the limit.

Finishing pays the prize for your position and records your driving progress. Entry fees, fuel and wear are real costs. Withdrawing does not refund the fee or return you and the car to the start. Being identified while racing can add to your original crime record; cooperating with the police does not cancel those consequences. The **journal** keeps your current race and latest result, while **statistics** records entries, wins, entry fees and prizes collected.

**Seasoned Driver**: Reach 600 driving experience and finish ten races to reduce baseline condition loss from driving cars by **10%**, both on ordinary trips and in road races. Extra wear from hard driving and accidents is unchanged. Bicycles and motorcycles do not receive this benefit. The journal shows your progress towards the trait.

**First Across**: Finish every section of an open-class race and take first place for the first time. This feat records your result without adding a separate cash reward.

## The smuggler's pub

Discover the pub through the original underground route before using its new daily menu. Have tea, rest, listen to news, help tidy up, or play cash blackjack and hold'em. These tables use cash; Connudatus Street casino chips stay with the casino.

Tea costs <span class="gold">£2</span>, takes five minutes and is limited to three cups per day. Rest for fifteen minutes, once per hour; listening to news is once per day. Tidying takes fifteen minutes, once per day, and pays <span class="gold">£4</span> on completion. Deal with an original task or character event before returning to these activities.

## Weapons and handheld items

The weapon shop uses the clothing shop's purchase, durability and equipment controls, with only handheld weapons for sale. Its range includes a small knife, stun device, crowbar, machete, handgun, shotgun, submachine gun and rifle. Manage both held items in the same handheld category; items sharing a functional tag cannot be equipped together.

Weapon actions appear in **combat against human attackers**. The relevant arm must be usable and the target still fighting. Carrying a weapon does not make its action available in every encounter.

| Firearm        | Rounds per action | Ammunition         |
| -------------- | ----------------- | ------------------ |
| Handgun        | 1                 | Handgun ammunition |
| Submachine gun | 3                 | Handgun ammunition |
| Rifle          | 2                 | Rifle ammunition   |
| Shotgun        | 1                 | Shotgun shells     |

With fewer rounds than a full burst, you can still fire; damage uses the number actually fired. Damage combines a fixed amount with a proportion of the target's maximum health. Shooting experience improves effectiveness and pain reduces it, within a 60%–100% range. Firing adds native assault crime, and a wounded bounty target needs medical attention before handover is complete.

While you hold a firearm in either hand, the weapon section of the characteristics page shows your existing shooting practice record and current damage multiplier, including the effect of pain.

## Landry's jobs

The daily selection aims for three legal jobs and one illegal job. Standing, location and module requirements can reduce the visible selection. Hospital supplies, books, food, garage parts, uniforms and documents are among the deliveries; stolen goods and drug transport are also available. Read the origin, destination, reward, deadline and risk before accepting.

1. Take the job and collect its cargo at the named location. Shortages, disputed fees or paperwork problems may allow inspection, waiting or negotiation.
2. Carry it on foot or load it into a nearby vehicle with enough cargo space. Loaded goods travel with that vehicle; arriving without it does not let you deliver its load.
3. Unload and deliver during the receiver's opening hours, then return for payment where required. Accepting or delivering a job alone is not recorded as money received.
4. Return the cargo or cancel if you cannot continue, accepting the consequences shown. Illegal cargo may bring searches, seizure or conflict; ordinary deliveries can also go wrong.

Late delivery normally pays 75%. Unresolved shortages and price disputes also reduce payment, and both reductions can apply. Theft of a loaded vehicle, seized cargo and an expired grace period can prevent completion.

## Bounties and being hunted

After agreeing to work with Grant, the board offers cases according to trust and accessible locations. Each lead lasts 72 hours. Investigate the sighting, then confirm the target's identity. Persuade, pursue or enter native combat when a confrontation begins. A car must be nearby, with space, fuel and the required driving qualification; it cannot appear at the station with a target by itself.

After handover, collect payment at the police station between 08:00 and 18:00. Follow the medical handover instructions for a gunshot casualty. Your criminal record may prevent taking or being paid for a case; police work does not erase it.

After confirming the target's identity, you may instead give the verified lead to Grant at the bounty desk while on duty, 08:00–18:00. Police take over the pursuit, paying **20%** of the base reward and increasing trust by 1. This closes your assignment. Lead fees do not count as captures or case-type progress, receive no bounty hunter bonus, and cannot be followed by a capture payment for that same assignment.

**Bounty Hunter** requires at least one actual payment from each of the current twelve case types. The board and journal show completed types and missing names. Repeating one type does not complete another. The final qualifying case pays its base reward; subsequent payments gain **15%** while you remain in cooperation with Grant. Your first verified and paid handover also earns **In the Net**.

You may take Landry's work and hunt wanted targets, but Landry and Grant are not allies. With a serious criminal record, other bounty hunters may pursue you in town. Negotiating, fleeing, driving away and resisting have their own requirements and consequences.

## Bailey and Mickey

Bailey's jobs, confiscations and threats connect with factories and the existing rent relationship. Look for the relevant options at the original office or flats. Negotiation depends on English skill, economic standing or established independence. Pay, refuse, seek help or resist through the choices offered; street standing alone does not remove an old debt.

Mickey helps check information, division of work and risks while retaining the original fame jobs. Two copies of a rumour are not two reliable sources. Mickey does not automatically join an outside operation or agree to Grant's or Landry's terms for you.

## The bank heist

The target is the **financial centre bank**. Reach at least 50 street standing and meet Landry and Wren. Landry must substantially trust your work and Wren needs at least 20 affection. Native blackbox and fencing experience can also establish your credentials with Landry. Arrange a private meeting, then take the normal route to the smuggler's pub and continue preparations between 09:00 and 18:00.

1. Discuss the plan with Landry and Wren, choosing a quiet or openly forceful approach.
2. Check bank information, prepare equipment and rehearse. English, maths, science, housekeeping, skulduggery and physical condition affect quality. A working factory with a packaging line can help with supplies.
3. Interview and hire one scout and one carrier. Advances and later shares are separate; withdrawing does not refund pay already given. Ability, injuries and whether a hire stays all matter.
4. Check preparation, crew and any selected vehicle before starting. Once there, you may still give up, take less or change your escape choice. Stress, fatigue, pain, alcohol and drugs impair performance.
5. Settle the split after escape to close the operation. Total loot, your share and preparation expenses are different; the recorded share is not net profit.

During preparation, you may contact Grant to report the plan or help apprehend the crew, and may change whom you provide information to. Decisions alter trust and future cooperation. Conflicting demands from the two sides cannot both be completed.

An incident suspends bank counter service for two days without cancelling accounts or loans. Native police consequences remain. A quiet success earns **Quiet as a Mouse** only after escape and the actual split, without raising the alarm or being identified. Entering unnoticed alone is insufficient.

Preparations keep their existing durations: 20 minutes at the bank counter, 20–30 minutes for supplies, 30 minutes for rehearsal, 15 minutes for introductions, and 5 minutes each for negotiation and hiring. Insufficient opening time is explained before work begins. Interrupted work records the time actually spent without charging for or awarding skills from an incomplete arrangement. Every arrangement needs to finish before closing.

After a successful split, the original crew members who remain free and fit for work can introduce ordinary orders at the smuggler's pub. The scout's warehouse supplies order has a <span class="gold">£120</span> base fee; the carrier's harbour delivery has a <span class="gold">£160</span> base fee. Take the actual route to collect and transport the goods, obtain the signed receipt, then collect payment from Landry on Harvest Street.

Your colleague's skill raises the total quote by 0–20%. They receive 25%, and you receive the remaining 75%; the quote is saved when you accept. Each colleague can introduce one order per day, and cancelling still uses that introduction. Late or disputed deliveries reduce both payments as usual. Each successfully paid order adds 10 to the colleague's skill for the corresponding role, up to 1000. This new income does not clear the criminal record from the bank job.
