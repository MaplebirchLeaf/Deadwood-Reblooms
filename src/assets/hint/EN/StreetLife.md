## Farm-road races

Visit the first road section outside Harvest Street at night to ask about the races, watch a start or enter your own car. The route follows the road to the farmland, with novice and open classes. Ordinary cars can enter. Snow cancels racing, while rain makes it easier to lose grip. Deal with any road encounter before approaching the gathering.

Check the entry fee, prizes and your vehicle before paying. Choose **Road out of town** in your car's travel menu and drive to the start before approaching the racers. A car left on Harvest Street or in a garage cannot enter remotely. Finish passenger and delivery work first. Driving skill, vehicle performance and decisions on the road all matter, so buying a more expensive car does not guarantee a win.

The novice entry fee is <span class="gold">£25</span>, with prizes of <span class="gold">£90 / £35 / £10</span> for the first three places. The open class requires 300 driving experience and costs <span class="gold">£100</span>, with prizes of <span class="gold">£300 / £150 / £50</span>. Entry takes two minutes, followed by twenty-one minutes for an uninterrupted run. Your car needs at least 45% condition and enough fuel for the whole course.

Choose whether to drive steadily, build speed or push harder at each stage. Steady driving is easier on the car; taking risks may gain places or cause a mistake. Traffic, bends and rain affect those choices. Each class allows one entry per night, including a run you withdraw from. A new racing night begins at 4 am; midnight does not reset the limit.

Finishing pays the prize for your position and records your driving progress. Entry fees, fuel and wear are real costs. Withdrawing does not refund the fee or return you and the car to the start. Being identified while racing can add to your original crime record; cooperating with the police does not cancel those consequences. The **journal** keeps your current race and latest result, while **statistics** records entries, wins, entry fees and prizes collected.

**Seasoned Driver**: Reach 600 driving experience and finish ten races to reduce baseline condition loss from driving cars by **10%**, both on ordinary trips and in road races. Extra wear from hard driving and accidents is unchanged. Bicycles and motorcycles do not receive this benefit. The journal shows your progress towards the trait.

**First Across**: Finish every section of an open-class race and take first place for the first time.

## The smuggler's pub

Discover the pub through the original underground route before using its new daily menu. Have tea, rest, listen to news, help tidy up, or play cash blackjack and hold'em. These tables use cash; Connudatus Street casino chips stay with the casino.

Tea costs <span class="gold">£2</span>, takes five minutes and is limited to three cups per day. Rest for fifteen minutes, once per hour; listening to news is once per day. Tidying takes fifteen minutes, once per day, and pays <span class="gold">£4</span> on completion. Deal with an original task or character event before returning to these activities.

## Weapons and handheld items

The weapon shop uses the clothing shop's purchase, durability and equipment controls, with only handheld weapons for sale. Its range includes a small knife, stun device, crowbar, machete, handgun, shotgun, submachine gun and rifle. Manage both held items in the same handheld category; items sharing a functional tag cannot be equipped together.

The weapon shop also sells pepper spray charges for £2500 each. Each restores one use. You cannot buy a charge while the canister is full.

Weapon actions appear in **combat against human attackers**. The relevant arm must be usable and the target still fighting.

| Firearm        | Rounds per action | Ammunition         |
| -------------- | ----------------- | ------------------ |
| Handgun        | 1                 | Handgun ammunition |
| Submachine gun | 3                 | Handgun ammunition |
| Rifle          | 2                 | Rifle ammunition   |
| Shotgun        | 1                 | Shotgun shells     |

With fewer rounds than a full burst, you can still fire; damage uses the number actually fired. Damage combines a fixed amount with a proportion of the target's maximum health. Shooting experience improves effectiveness and pain reduces it, within a 60%–100% range. Firing adds native assault crime, and a wounded bounty target needs medical attention before handover is complete.

While you hold a firearm in either hand, the weapon section of the characteristics page shows your existing shooting practice record and current damage multiplier, including the effect of pain.

## Landry's jobs

The daily selection aims for three legal jobs and one illegal job. Standing, location and module requirements can reduce the visible selection. Hospital supplies, books, food, garage parts, uniforms and documents are among the deliveries; stolen goods and drug transport are also available. Read the origin, destination, reward, deadline and risk before accepting.

1. Take the job and collect its cargo at the named location. Shortages, disputed fees or paperwork problems may allow inspection, waiting or negotiation.
2. Carry it on foot or load it into a nearby vehicle with enough cargo space. Loaded goods travel with that vehicle; arriving without it does not let you deliver its load.
3. Unload and deliver during the receiver's opening hours, then return for payment where required. Accepting or delivering a job alone is not recorded as money received.
4. Return the cargo or cancel if you cannot continue, accepting the consequences shown. Illegal cargo may bring searches, seizure or conflict; ordinary deliveries can also go wrong.

Drinks for the upstairs gambling room, pub uniforms, cafe supplies and dockside tools each have a one-in-four chance of someone causing trouble: a patron blocking the stairs, persistent unwanted attention, or a stranger demanding a handling charge. Wait where staff can see you for ten minutes, or use 400 Skulduggery to slip through the crowd in five. You can still leave or return the shipment. Clearing the obstruction does not reduce your fee, though time lost can make a delivery late.

Late delivery normally pays 75%. Unresolved shortages and price disputes also reduce payment, and both reductions can apply. Theft of a loaded vehicle, seized cargo and an expired grace period can prevent completion.

## The three smaller arrangements

Discover the smuggler's pub, meet Landry and reach 20 street standing to ask about people and work between 09:00 and 18:00. Landry must be willing to talk: at least 20 affection, twenty-five completed original blackbox tasks, or <span class="gold">£1,000</span> actually earned through fencing. You also need any one of these: two paid street jobs, three completed original blackbox tasks, or <span class="gold">£1,000</span> earned through fencing. Old results will not persuade Landry if you have already fallen out.

Ask Landry for introductions and arrange one scout and one carrier, then check **unfinished business** at the pub. The three arrangements follow one another. Make your own way between the locations; the journal keeps your next destination. Meetings at the pub, docks, industrial alleyways and Ocean Breeze café run from 09:00 to 18:00, and each step needs enough time to finish.

| Arrangement                 | Route                                                                  | What you do                                                                            |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The missing crate**       | Pub → docks → industrial alleyways → pub                               | Check paperwork and seals, resolve a disputed delivery, then settle up                 |
| **A load worth leaving**    | Pub → industrial alleyways → docks → pub                               | Work with Wren and decide what to do when the load and a companion both need attention |
| **Where the story started** | Pub → Ocean Breeze café → industrial alleyways or police station → pub | Separate eyewitness accounts from retellings and check another source                  |

After settling Landry's delivery, meet Wren and reach 20 affection to hear about the second job. Having worked with Wren on the original mansion job gives you some shared experience here.

Once Wren's arrangement is settled, you can look into the rumours. Mickey can help if you have already met and can speak at the pub; **you can investigate on your own without meeting Mickey**. If you know Grant, have agreed to cooperate and can find the detective on duty, the old case can also be checked at the station. Otherwise, seek an independent source in the alleyways. Asking about the old case does not commit you to reporting another job or remove your own criminal record.

None of these jobs needs a perfect result. A reduced delivery, a load left behind or uncertainty honestly recorded can still bring an arrangement to a close. Your payment and later plans reflect those choices. Return to give your account and settle up; accepting a job alone does not count towards opening the bank operation.

To change the crew, return to **unfinished business** at the pub and spend five minutes choosing **Put the arrangement on hold**. You can pause even if a hire cannot currently take part. Your current step, findings, decisions and expenses are kept. Once you have arranged an available scout and carrier, spend another five minutes choosing **Agree on the crew and continue**, then pick up at the unfinished step. Earlier checks are not rolled again, and payments or experience already earned are not awarded twice. Pausing does not count as completion, and a settled arrangement cannot be reopened.

## Crew and ongoing work

Landry introduces the same three people. Their names and appearances, separate starting abilities and individual growth rates are set at that introduction. Looking again does not replace them. Scouting and carrying are separate abilities, so consider both before choosing by price.

| Candidate                 | Starting range for each ability | Growth range for each ability |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| A first-time hire         | 150–300                         | 110%–145%                     |
| A practiced hand          | 350–550                         | 90%–120%                      |
| An experienced specialist | 550–750                         | 75%–105%                      |

Work improves each participant's assigned role, up to 1000. Individual growth changes the amount: 10 base experience gives 12 points at a 120% growth rate. The other ability does not increase alongside it. Injuries reduce effectiveness, and someone unable to take part or held in custody cannot gain experience.

**Familiarity belongs to the particular pair**, from 0 to 100. A different partner uses that pair's own experience together; returning to an earlier partner restores the familiarity they already shared. Joint work builds it. In the final heist, it can add up to **15%** to their role contributions, alongside **Crew Leader's 10%**. Your own abilities and condition still affect the checks.

The first hire costs an advance, which is not refunded on dismissal. Someone whose advance is already paid can be invited back without another payment. Changing a person or exchanging roles invalidates the old rehearsal. Their abilities and shared familiarity remain, but rehearsing the same people in the same roles again gives no further experience. Keep the agreed crew during an active arrangement; pause at the pub before changing it.

## Bounties and being hunted

After agreeing to work with Grant, the board offers cases according to trust and accessible locations. Each lead lasts 72 hours. Investigate the sighting, then confirm the target's identity. Persuade, pursue or enter native combat when a confrontation begins. A car must be nearby, with space, fuel and the required driving qualification; it cannot appear at the station with a target by itself.

After handover, collect payment at the police station between 08:00 and 18:00. Follow the medical handover instructions for a gunshot casualty. Your criminal record may prevent taking or being paid for a case; police work does not erase it.

After confirming the target's identity, you may instead give the verified lead to Grant at the bounty desk while on duty, 08:00–18:00. Police take over the pursuit, paying **20%** of the base reward and increasing trust by 1. This closes your assignment. Lead fees do not count as captures or case-type progress, receive no bounty hunter bonus, and cannot be followed by a capture payment for that same assignment.

**Bounty Hunter** requires at least one actual payment from each of the current twelve case types. The board and journal show completed types and missing names. Repeating one type does not complete another. The final qualifying case pays its base reward; subsequent payments gain **15%** while you remain in cooperation with Grant. Your first verified and paid handover also earns **In the Net**.

You may take Landry's work and hunt wanted targets, but Landry and Grant are not allies. With a serious criminal record, other bounty hunters may pursue you in town. Before being spotted, you can try to slip away using skulduggery. If caught, you can still negotiate, run or resist. Evading the hunter leaves your evidence intact, but this street pursuit will not trigger again that day.

## Bailey and Mickey

Bailey's jobs, confiscations and threats connect with factories and the existing rent relationship. Look for the relevant options at the original office or flats. Negotiation depends on English skill, economic standing or established independence. Pay, refuse, seek help or resist through the choices offered; street standing alone does not remove an old debt.

## The bank heist

The target is the **financial centre bank**. Starting a new operation requires all three arrangements above to be completed and settled, Finance enabled and 70 street standing. Meet Landry and Wren; Wren needs at least 20 affection. Landry needs substantial confidence in your work: 60 affection, twenty-five completed original blackbox tasks, or <span class="gold">£8,000</span> actually earned through fencing. Any one qualifies, but a confirmed betrayal still prevents that trust. Arrange a private meeting, then take the normal route to the smuggler's pub and continue preparations between 09:00 and 18:00.

1. Discuss the plan with Landry and Wren. A newly accepted bank arrangement first opens **the harbour pay chest** and **the civic reception**. Visit each scene and report its actual outcome at the pub.
2. Choose a quiet or openly forceful approach, check bank information, prepare equipment and rehearse. English, maths, science, housekeeping, skulduggery and physical condition affect quality. A working factory with a packaging line can help with supplies.
3. Confirm your scout, carrier, roles and shares. Advances and later shares are separate; withdrawing does not refund pay already given. Ability, injuries and the pair's familiarity all matter.
4. Once both jobs are reported and preparation and crew are ready, begin at the High Street financial centre bank counter, Tuesday–Saturday, 09:00–17:00. The hall, inner passage, vault, carrying and escape each need actual decisions; confrontations use native combat. Once the operation has begun, the counter closing does not by itself interrupt the scene. Stress, fatigue, pain, alcohol and drugs impair performance.
5. The haul is gold. After escape, deal with the missing crew and sale arrangements, wait until the agreed date, sell it and settle the split. The gold estimate, sale proceeds, your collected share and recorded expenses are separate amounts.

### The bank and its gold

Ordinary supplies cost <span class="gold">£1,500</span>, with another <span class="gold">£300</span> for rehearsal. Your own operating packaging factory can reduce supplies to <span class="gold">£750</span>. The three candidates charge advances of <span class="gold">£300 / £900 / £1,800</span>; hire two of them. Each also takes the agreed share after the job. Mickey charges <span class="gold">£600</span> to review one part of the plan. The preparation page keeps recorded expenses, and settlement shows what remains of your share after those costs.

**Crew Leader**: Hire an available scout and carrier, then complete a rehearsal at quality 8 or higher. Their skill contributions to scouting, carrying and escape increase by **10%**. Injuries still reduce their effectiveness; a missing hire or someone in custody cannot provide the bonus. Read the preparation quality and current estimates before deciding who does which job.

Work through the hall and inner passage before dealing with the vault guards, carrying gold and regrouping with the crew. Warning the clerk or leading customers clear protects bystanders but gives up an unnoticed entry. A loading route actually inspected during the dock case provides another entrance. Adrian’s presence, familiarity and whether your face is covered affect recognition and relationship consequences. After a setback, you can still talk, resist, abandon the haul or involve the police.

Taking more gold makes escape harder. At the regrouping point, actually check who is injured or missing: search, use packed dressings, or leave some gold to help someone walk. People you explicitly leave behind stop contributing to the escape. Beyond the street exit, find cover or shake the pursuit, then leave on foot or in the prepared car. A careful departure sacrifices part of the gold and takes longer. Walking requires freedom to move; driving also requires the car actually on High Street, enough fuel and passenger seats, and a fit, licensed driver. A failed escape can lose the haul and leave you or the hires hurt or detained. Paid expenses and criminal evidence remain.

The bars can be handed over from the agreed release day, normally the second day after escape. Resolve the contacts’ whereabouts, then arrange a buyer at the smugglers’ pub and attend on the appointed day, 09:00–18:00. A missed appointment needs rearranging and reduces the offer. Before handing over the gold you can still try the other buyer.

An inspection does not automatically pay the estimated value. Science checks the weights and metal, maths checks the accounts and obtains reliable paperwork, and English allows one further price negotiation. A failed negotiation can lower the offer. Recorded expenses include preparation for the earlier cases and later investigation and rescue costs. The bank share less those expenses is not the whole chain’s net profit: shares from the earlier cases were paid separately.

### Published evidence and prices

An actual public dock incident affects Mer Street Shipping; the bank incident and reopening affect High Street Centre. Actively publishing evidence tied to the financial centre’s entrusted accounts and warehouse guarantee numbers adds news for that identified security. Private inquiries, sealing a copy and ordinary rescues do not move every listed share. Publishing account numbers also makes the gold harder to place, so the buyer discounts it further. The civic reception has no established issuer; unknown papers do not invent corporate misconduct or a price move. Each event is recorded once. Rebooking, rereading news or retrying a failed earlier case cannot keep generating the same market event.

During preparation, you may contact Grant to report the plan or help apprehend the crew, and may change whom you provide information to. **An agreement to help is not immunity**: your own outstanding case may still mean detention and prevent collecting police payment. Reporting the crew at the last minute also leaves your own offences intact. Decisions alter trust and future cooperation. Conflicting demands from the two sides cannot both be completed.

An incident suspends bank counter service for two days without cancelling accounts or loans. Native police consequences remain. A quiet success earns **Quiet as a Mouse** only after escape and the actual split, without raising the alarm or being identified. Entering unnoticed alone is insufficient.

Preparations keep their existing durations: 20 minutes at the bank counter, 20–30 minutes for supplies, 30 minutes for rehearsal, 15 minutes for introductions, and 5 minutes each for negotiation and hiring. Insufficient opening time is explained before work begins. Interrupted work records the time actually spent without charging for or awarding skills from an incomplete arrangement. Every arrangement needs to finish before closing.

After a successful split, the original crew members who remain free and fit for work can introduce ordinary orders at the smuggler's pub. The scout's warehouse supplies order has a <span class="gold">£120</span> base fee; the carrier's harbour delivery has a <span class="gold">£160</span> base fee. Take the actual route to collect and transport the goods, obtain the signed receipt, then collect payment from Landry on Harvest Street.

Your colleague's skill raises the total quote by 0–20%. They receive 25%, and you receive the remaining 75%; the quote is saved when you accept. Each colleague can introduce one order per day, and cancelling still uses that introduction. Late or disputed deliveries reduce both payments as usual. Each successfully paid order gives 10 base experience, adjusted by that colleague's individual growth rate, to the assigned role only, up to 1000.

A public theft or confrontation in the harbour pay chest records one −2% Mer Street shipping notice. The bank incident and counter reopening affect High Street Shopping Centre. Paid bounties and legal supplies also bring related business notices. See **Finance and business → Banking and investing** for trading rules.
