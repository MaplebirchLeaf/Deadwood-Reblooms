### Unlock an orchard

With Orchard enabled, complete the land preparation event by day, suitably dressed, with at least one arm unbound and stress below its maximum:

| Place                     | First requirement                                                                    | Initial and maximum plots                    |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| Behind the temple garden  | Monk or priest rank and a fully cleared garden                                       | 5 initially, 6 maximum; one mature plum tree |
| Alex's farm               | Clear all nine original fields and ask while Alex handles cottage administration     | 3 initially, 12 maximum                      |
| Behind Darkfeather Shrine | Meet Tsukihan, reach 10 affection and ask about the trees in the courtyard or garden | 2 fixed plots; a mature apple and plum tree  |

Initial preparation at the temple or farm takes an hour. Later, **Continue clearing** works in 40-minute sessions; each new temple plot needs 80 minutes total, and a farm plot 120. Progress can be completed over several visits. The shrine ground is already prepared: check the trees with Tsukihan for 10 minutes to begin.

About half the temple harvest is yours; the kitchen receives the rest and grants grace. Farm and shrine harvests belong to you. Temple access still needs the relevant rank, while farm attacks must be handled first. Shrine work requires Tsukihan to remain active and affection to stay at 10 or above.

### Find seeds, plant and care

Original picking can reveal apple, pear, peach, plum, orange and lemon seeds. Lime seeds come from collecting limes in the temple garden; cherry seeds cost <span class="gold">£1,000</span> at the supermarket. **Unlock each seed source once; later planting does not consume more seeds.**

Select a species and drag a tool onto a plot; on mobile, tap the tool and then the plot. Plant in cleared empty plots, and tap a plot to check water, fertiliser, soil and growth.

- **Water**: restore seven days of moisture when dry. Rain replenishes it, and irrigating all nine original farm fields also supplies the farm orchard automatically. Tsukihan tends the two shrine plots while active; that care stops during an absence.
- **Young trees**: one bag of fertiliser lasts 30 days and doubles growth while watered. Dry trees stop growing; fertiliser cannot replace water.
- **Mature trees**: C-grade plant care allows soil improvement for better yield. It normally expires with the fertiliser, while Green Thumb retains the improvement permanently.
- **Harvest**: collect all ripe fruit on that tree. Removing a tree requires confirmation and loses its fruit, while cleared land remains available.

Fertiliser shares the original field supply. Risk stealing it while searching dock cargo at night once you can identify it, or buy an occasional <span class="gold">£1,000</span> bag from the special market's gardening stall.

### Maturity, seasons and blood lemons

Most trees mature after 28 watered growth days, cherries after 26 and limes after 31. Mature trees produce a batch every seven watered days and retain at most three batches. Production pauses when full; harvesting frees space.

| Tree          | Productive seasons     |
| ------------- | ---------------------- |
| Apple, pear   | Autumn                 |
| Peach, orange | Summer                 |
| Plum          | Summer, autumn         |
| Cherry        | Spring                 |
| Lemon, lime   | Spring, summer, autumn |

Other seasons, including winter, yield half the productive-season amount. Plant care, soil and age also matter; three and nine successful harvests respectively reach higher yield stages.

New fruit produced by a lemon tree during a blood moon becomes **blood lemons**. Existing lemons remain unchanged, and blood lemons remain after dawn. Harvest a full three-batch tree in advance to make room.

Each barrel uses **20 matching fruit**, costs <span class="gold">£5</span> to prepare and takes 30 minutes of work. Once fermented, bottle it into **8 bottles** for <span class="gold">£4</span> in glass and 15 minutes of work. Fruit reserves and unexpired delivery commitments stay in stock; matching fruit gathered elsewhere can also be used.

Bottle **80 bottles** and either sell **80 bottles wholesale** or supply **24 bottles** to Robin's drinks shop to earn **Cellar Hand**. Bottling a batch by hand then takes ten minutes.

After earning Cellar Hand, spend <span class="gold">£600</span> on a powered press and bottling bench, with one hour to fit them. Each batch uses twenty fruit, <span class="gold">£5</span> of processing supplies and <span class="gold">£4</span> of bottles, with the fermentation times listed above. Production and deliveries use stock beyond reserves and unexpired orders. Shortages of money, spare fruit or barrel space pause work until the next shift. Automatic deliveries need Robin at the shop to receive the stock: six bottles per trip, at most twelve a day, with <span class="gold">£5</span> freight taken directly from each trip's payment. The shop needs enough joint working funds to pay for the bottles, and the cabinet holds twenty-four. Manual deliveries have their usual rules and do not share the automatic twelve-bottle limit. Changes after 09:00 start the next day; switching work off is immediate. A farm attack pauses that day's production. The journal and statistics show equipment, recipe, work and reasons for pauses.

### A drink at your property

Keep bottled orchard wine in stock to open it in your property's sitting room, lounge, kitchen or wine cellar. Original red and white wine remain available. Choose a resident lover who is at home, or drink alone. One bottle takes 20 minutes; drunkenness and sensitivity follow the original rules. The first drink with that lover each day adds one affection.

Afterwards, keep talking, put the glasses away, or spend some time close together.

### Later automation and transport

With Finance enabled and a debit card, authorise bank wage renewal and hire a carrier for morning deliveries. Both require your approval. Insufficient funds pause them, without backdated wages for missed work.

Transport pays 70% of the sale price within the shared 100-fruit daily limit, charging <span class="gold">£5</span> per trip and depositing net takings into the bank. It sends fruit beyond reserves and pending orders; deliver blood lemons yourself. A farm attack pauses that day's tending, picking and transport. Work resumes with the next shift, and the worker leaves after seven unpaid days. Dismissing the orchard worker cancels renewal and transport arrangements.

### Remy: begin at the riding school

With Orchard enabled, choose **Approach Remy** at the riding school during the day. Avoid closing time, combat and emergencies.

After three ordinary conversations, Remy appears among the important relationships on the Social page. Agree to start dating before choosing Remy as a love interest.

1. Have an ordinary conversation once daily for 10 minutes. Teaching and care topics build familiarity; confronting farm conflict, the mask or captivity is separate and does not count towards relationship conversations.
2. Give one stocked dish or fruit daily. Scones are a favourite, and reserved orchard fruit is not gifted.
3. After three conversations and 20 affection, arrive early to suggest 45 minutes alone, once daily. Tea is indoors. Outside winter, you can also walk by the fence when there is no rain or snow.
4. After at least five conversations, two dates and 30 affection, discuss dating explicitly. Agreement makes Remy eligible; select Remy on the Attitudes page afterwards.

Once familiar, spend 15 minutes daily resting, checking tack or reviewing lessons. Once dating and after two dates, you can have a pasture picnic outside winter when there is no rain or snow. Ending the relationship requires confirmation and removes the selected love interest; ordinary conversation and food gifts remain possible.

### Visiting Remy's house

After beginning an ordinary relationship, visit the estate at night once the riding school has closed and ask the guard to announce you. Leave at least 20 minutes to finish before eleven. The gate remains closed to you after a refusal or while security is on alert. Remy also cannot receive visitors while away at your banquet or when either of you is going into labour.

Keep Remy company in the drawing room, discuss work in the study, or ask Remy to leave Alex alone once you know about the farm conflict. These choices share the riding school's once-daily activity, add 1 affection, and drawing-room company also relieves stress.

### Captivity route and farm conflict

Call Remy back after a captive visit to ask questions or end a relationship.

You may ask Remy to remain for five more minutes once daily, adding 20 attachment, 1 affection and 2 obedience. At 50 attachment, 60 original obedience and 25 affection, explicitly choosing to stay can grant **Stockholm Syndrome: Remy** and love-interest eligibility.

Tell Alex about your contact with Remy in the farm orchard. Alex will respond, and trust may change.
