### Original cheat menu

The sidebar offers the original cheat menu even when cheats have not been enabled in the current game. Opening the menu alone does not enable cheats; individual actions still follow the original rules.

Use Cheat Collection to save and repeatedly execute your own commands.

### Encounter values

Encounters that display NPC status add numbers beside the coloured descriptions:

- Health, arousal and anger: current value / maximum.
- Trust: current value.

Special scenes with their own interface may not show every value.
