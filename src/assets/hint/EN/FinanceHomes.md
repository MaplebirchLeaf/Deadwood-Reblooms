Buy a home first, then select that property from the furniture-shop entrance. Living there, inviting residents, renting it out and borrowing against it each have their own requirements. The journal lists bills, residents and upcoming obligations.

Credit may cover the deposit shortfall, but not the bank fee or reserve. Every non-rented, unfrozen home remains usable, and the orphanage room stays available.

### Property management

Hire a property manager at the property desk, using a debit card to authorise payments. The same person can manage several homes; assign each home separately after signing. Assignments start disabled. Ending the agreement cancels all assignments; returning brings back the same person. Weekly fees are 10% of the current assessed weekly rent, at least <span class="gold">£5</span>, including vacant and owner-occupied homes. Repairs cost extra at their existing prices. The manager follows up late rent after a week, repairs leaks and restores condition when it reaches 90. Missing staff, an unavailable debit card or insufficient funds suspend the affected work. They never borrow on credit, renovate, renew leases or purchase properties for you. Frozen and auction-listed properties suspend service; a sale cancels it.

Listed rents range from 0.22% to 0.32% of the price per week, before upkeep, repairs, vacancy and tenant problems. Renovation and good condition improve earnings. Management reduces manual work at the cost of lower net returns. The counter displays the latest fee, repair bill and rent recovered.

### Property-backed borrowing and tenant messages

At the **property counter**, select borrowing against an owned home. Condition and renovation affect the appraisal and lending limit. Only one property loan can be outstanding. Funds enter the bank less the fee; the home can still be occupied or rented, but cannot be auctioned voluntarily while pledged.

New lettings have a tenant-search period. Tenants may pay a week late or report a leak. Read their messages at the property counter. Wait for overdue rent or accept a reduced immediate payment. Leaks reduce net rent and damage condition until repaired.

<span class="red">A foreclosure auction does not erase debt exceeding its proceeds.</span> The report separates proceeds, returned cash and the outstanding shortfall.

### Why a resident is absent

Registration does not keep a character permanently at home. School, work, temple duties and vanilla stories still take priority. Belongings in a guest room establish residency, not current presence.

Check the living room first, then chat or invite one person upstairs. A double bed holds the PC and one partner. The other stays in the living or guest room according to the time. Joint conversation requires both residents currently present.

- Robin and Kylar refuse to live with Whitney. Reversing the invitation order does not bypass this conflict.

- Sydney and Kylar's former friendship does not erase their current distance or guarantee shared intimacy.

### Outdoor spaces and gliding

**Gliding** begins inside a high home's outside-view page. Suitable heights at Barb, Commercial, Cliff and Danube Street have entrances. Complete harpy form (stage six) can glide directly. Angel or demon stage six and fallen angel stage two require pain low enough to glide and learned flying or gliding. An untrained divine form with sufficient willpower can attempt a descent, but failure blocks another attempt that day and does not immediately unlock town flight. Town destinations and descending to ground level are separate choices, commonly taking ten and two minutes respectively. Exposure and vanilla exposure tolerance still restrict street landings.

**Planting** varies by property: one plot at the cottage, one loft planter and three manor growing spaces. Barb and Cliff Street homes do not provide equivalent plots. Check actual outdoor options rather than assuming every balcony supports planting.

### Rent buyouts and the donations desk

Visit Bailey's office between **7:00 and 10:00** and choose **Discuss ending your rent payments**. Buy out your own rent, Robin's rent, or both. Robin must read and sign the terms before you pay for that contract. You can settle your own separately. Begin payment **before 9:50**.

Each contract costs at least **<span class="gold">£250,000</span>**, or **260 weeks** of that person's current base rent if higher. Unpaid rent is additional. The quoted buyout stays fixed, while arrears are settled at payment. Cash is used first, with the remainder drawn from a bank account with a debit card.

<span class="green">A paid contract ends that person's rent collection. The original room and keys remain available, with the choice to move out.</span> Buying out only one leaves the other paying separately and ends the previous combined-rent arrangement. You can show Robin the receipt for Robin's contract afterwards.

**MSO: Mansion Street Orphanage** is also listed at the securities desk. Large rent settlements, collection disputes, donations and repair bills affect its quotes. Share ownership is an investment and does not alter living arrangements.

The **donations desk** is beside the town-hall front desk. Ask after waiting in line, on weekdays between **8:00 and 18:00**. Choose the orphanage, hospital, school or temple and donate with your name or anonymously. Each project displays its target and cumulative contributions. Recent receipts remain at the desk. Cash and debit-card bank funds pay into the selected project, with that record retained for future purchasing and related content.

Named contributions earn **5 kindness per accumulated <span class="gold">£1,000</span>**, up to each funding target. Signing for the delivery earns another **25 kindness**, and the follow-up meeting earns **10 social fame**. Once you have agreed arrangements with the recipient, the corresponding benefits become available:

- **School:** one minor detention review, equipment use after lessons, and one donor audience each week to discuss equipment arrangements again.
- **Temple:** one quiet-room rest per day, one private audience with Jordan each week, and a say in relief priorities. Members with the exhibitionism trait can separately request permission to go unclothed in the residential areas.
- **Hospital:** private parasite treatment has a **25% fee reduction**, with one health consultation each week.
- **Orphanage:** one **seven-day rent extension** and one scheduled private appointment each week.

After signing and following up, the four institutions take turns sending routine purchase enquiries to suitable factories. The orphanage and temple buy crates; the school and hospital buy shelving and lockers. At most one enquiry is issued across your factories each week, giving each institution a turn in a four-week cycle. Materials, wages, production, late delivery and invoices follow the existing factory accounts. Actual delivery earns **15 business fame**, with at least 28 days between awards from the same institution. Every **400 business fame** improves ordinary quote quality and reduces default risk, up to five tiers.

### Avery's adoption

**Discuss adoption** in Bailey's office lets you state your wishes in advance. Its appearance does not mean Avery has made an offer. Reading or leaving does not refuse adoption. After signing a refusal, you can return to withdraw it.

Buying out your own contract also blocks Avery from acquiring custody through Bailey. Withdrawing a refusal does not undo that buyout. Buying out only Robin's contract does not block your adoption storyline.

Neither refusal nor buying out your contract blocks the separate **director or controlling shareholder mansion invitation** described in [Business and governance](#deadwood-guide-FinanceBusiness). That route opens the mansion without adoption or Avery paying your rent.

The vanilla invitation also requires progress through Avery's or Landry's artefact storyline, Avery still active, no existing mansion storyline, and a visit to the orphanage outside nighttime. You need at least **50 affection**, **400 housekeeping**, **6 known recipes**, and **6 completed dates**, plus an adoption score of **130**. The score is **housekeeping ÷ 20 + affection ÷ 2 + recipes × 2 + dates × 3**. Meeting only the four minimums gives **75**.
