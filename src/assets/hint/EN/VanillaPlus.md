## Ghostbrew

Pick **ghostshrooms** in Kylar's manor grounds once a day, taking one hour. Bring one to Gwylan while they are at the shop to learn the recipe: one **ghostshroom, white wine and strange flower**. Prepare it in a native kitchen that allows these ingredients. Each bottle takes one hour.

Bring a bottle to a purchased home and select it from the wine menu in the sitting room, kitchen or wine cellar. Drinking takes twenty minutes. Each bottle adds **60 base alcohol** and **120 hallucinogen points**. Your tolerance affects how drunk you become, and the effects fade over time. A resident can keep you company.

## Six attribute breakthroughs

Reach the normal attribute ceiling and complete its challenges to break through. Then keep training to the new ceiling to earn the trait. **Statistics → Mod statistics → Attribute breakthroughs** shows current values, ceilings and the number of requirements met. The requirements are listed below. Some challenges only need completing once; others, such as makeup and ear slimes, depend on your condition at the time.

Willpower, physique and beauty rise to **125%** of their normal ceilings; exhibitionism, deviancy and promiscuity rise to **150%**. Reaching all six new ceilings also earns Master of All.

### Willpower

Reach maximum willpower, keep a slime in each ear, finish Schism and complete the full temple vigil.

In **the Ivory Wraith’s underwater prison in the lake ruins**, search for a way out, examine the cages, keep searching, then swim down the stairs. With every other requirement met, return to the prison’s main page and choose **Dive towards the rune again**.

### Physique

Reach maximum physique, escape a flats break-in encounter, experience the farm assault at stage **12** or later and a relevant farm confrontation, then escape the pound.

Use **200 skulduggery** to pick the lock of a room in the block of flats. Escape using physique, smash through the obstacle or win the resulting fight to complete this challenge. **Escape the pound** means escaping the animal pound.

### Beauty

Reach maximum beauty, once attain **7,000 base allure outdoors outside a blood moon**, and currently have at least **1,000 modelling fame**. Remove all makeup, coloured contacts and face masks before breaking through.

### Exhibitionism

Reach maximum exhibitionism and complete these two challenges:

- **School nude swimming:** ask to swim naked, then complete the recorded backstroke stage once the counter reaches **five**. Undressing at the pool alone does not count.
- **Exposed crossings:** earn the native **A Lewd Adventure** feat. Within the same day, cross Connudatus Street, cross High Street into the park, and cross the flyover to the industrial district — the last of the three is the bridge.

After earning the seventh-level exhibitionism trait, you can walk around town at a degree of exposure you are already comfortable with. Exposure can still affect fame and attract encounters.

### Deviancy

Reach maximum deviancy and obtain **Wildsong** through Gwylan’s ritual.

The seventh-level deviancy trait, Beyond Taboo, lets you cultivate several animal transformations together without their growth points naturally decaying. Develop each form through its own activities and thresholds. Choose which parts to show in the mirror.

### Promiscuity

Promiscuity, exhibitionism and deviancy must all reach their **normal ceilings** at the same time.

## Taste for Cruelty

**Unlock**: Enable VanillaPlus and earn **Sadomasochist** in the **current save** by reaching the highest sadism and masochism levels together. Saves that already earned the feat receive <span class="def">Taste for Cruelty</span> immediately.

**Effects**: <span class="def">Your sadism and masochism stop fading naturally each day</span>. In Attitudes, leave “Reflecting on the pain you have given and received” unchecked to retain them, or check it to resume natural decay.

## Iron Will

Once your fallen angel form reaches its stable stage, Iron Will prevents willpower decay. The attitude “Being overwhelmed by pleasure” is checked by default and caps arousal accumulated during everyday activities at **1 below the orgasm threshold**, preventing an unexpected climax. Uncheck it to allow the normal threshold without losing willpower protection. Masturbation, combat and Gwylan’s ritual always retain the native threshold.

## Trinity

**Prerequisites**: Enable VanillaPlus and divine transformations in the game settings. Pray in the temple's prayer room and encounter each of the three vanilla presences: warmth, stone and empty darkness. Both embracing a presence and snapping out of it count as experiencing that contact. Your fallen angel form must have **settled into its stable stage**, and you must wear a **dark pendant** when praying.

**Where and when**: Once all three contacts have been recorded, wear a dark pendant during a solar eclipse and use the native **Sneak inside** option at the prayer-room entrance, then pray. This entrance requires the vanilla history level and costs grace. Each prayer has a **10%** chance of a new encounter. The eclipse must still be active when that prayer finishes.

**Choices**: Choose **Embrace** to take a vanilla willpower check. Higher willpower makes it easier to remain conscious, and the option displays the difficulty. Success still causes pain, stress and trauma, increases awareness and grants **Trinity**. Failure brings a stronger shock and reduces control. You can try again during a later eclipse. **Snap out of it** lets you leave, gaining a little stress, awareness and willpower.

**Trait**: Trinity lets angel, fallen angel and demon forms coexist, prevents their development points from naturally decaying and removes the fallen angel’s daily purity loss. Each day, maximum purity grants angel points and zero purity grants demon points.

**Appearance**: Choose each developed form separately at a mirror.

## Eerie mirrors: entry and return

1. Complete the deviancy breakthrough and reach its new maximum to gain the stage-seven trait.
2. First touch a mirror you want to use. Mirrors at the orphanage, owned homes, farm, tower, temple and some character homes are available. Get permission before entering a character’s home.
3. At game time **03:00 until before 04:00**, with tentacles enabled and either hallucinations stage two or the day's mirror-tentacle condition, check for crossing the eerie mirror.
4. Watch for mirror hints, exits and dangers on the tentacle plains. Mirror locations change each time you enter.
5. Returning through a different mirror from the entry origin awards **Beyond the Mirror**. When mirrors share nearby coordinates, the actual exit selected matters.
6. Leaving through the entry mirror on the plains or collapsing there **returns you to the mirror you entered through**. Collapsing in labour follows the vanilla mirror-entry route to the hospital.

The orphanage mirror occasionally offers **Touch the strange reflection**. Read the warning about lasting consequences before touching it, or walk away.

## Hospital services

**Virginity restoration** is offered in the hospital foyer for eligible anatomy, at <span class="gold">£1,000</span> and about one hour per procedure. Body state and chastity equipment restrict eligibility.

## Gargantuan size and multiplayer actions

The PC gains **size 7: Gargantuan**, through existing body-size settings and growth routes. The sidebar has the matching appearance, and NPC size settings also gain a corresponding option.

After the promiscuity breakthrough, try extra actions in encounters with suitable participants, available body parts and positions. Reaching the new ceiling grants its trait.
