## Six attribute breakthroughs

Reach the normal attribute ceiling and complete its challenges to break through. Then keep training to the new ceiling to earn the trait. **Statistics → Deadwood Reblooms: progress** shows what you have done and what remains. Some challenges only need completing once; others, such as makeup and ear slimes, depend on your condition at the time.

Willpower, physique and beauty rise to **125%** of their normal ceilings; exhibitionism, deviancy and promiscuity rise to **150%**. Reaching all six new ceilings also earns Incorrigible.

### Willpower

Reach maximum willpower, keep a slime in each ear, finish Schism and complete the full temple vigil.

In **the Ivory Wraith’s underwater prison in the lake ruins**, search for a way out, examine the cages, keep searching, then swim down the stairs to reach search step **4**. With every other requirement met, return to the prison’s main page and choose **Dive towards the rune again**.

### Physique

Reach maximum physique, escape a flats break-in encounter, experience the farm assault at stage **12** or later and a relevant farm confrontation, then escape the pound.

The break-in takes place in **a locked flat in the native block of flats**, not a home you bought. Picking the lock requires **200 skulduggery**. A successful physique escape, smashing through the obstacle or winning the resulting fight counts. The **pound** is the animal pound, not the asylum.

### Beauty

Reach maximum beauty, once attain **7,000 base allure outdoors outside a blood moon**, and currently have at least **1,000 modelling fame**. Remove all makeup, coloured contacts and face masks before breaking through. Base allure and total allure are different values; Statistics shows which requirements you meet.

### Exhibitionism

Reach maximum exhibitionism and complete these two challenges:

- **School nude swimming:** ask to swim naked, then complete the recorded backstroke stage once the counter reaches **five**. Undressing at the pool alone does not count.
- **Exposed crossings:** earn the native **A Lewd Adventure** feat. Within the same day, cross Connudatus Street, cross High Street into the park, and cross the flyover to the industrial district — the last of the three is the bridge.

After earning the seventh-level exhibitionism trait, you can walk around town naked if you are already comfortable with that degree of exposure. Fame, encounters and other consequences still apply.

### Deviancy

Reach maximum deviancy and obtain **Wildsong** through Gwylan’s ritual.

The seventh-level deviancy trait, Beyond Taboo, lets you cultivate several animal transformations together. They no longer cancel one another out or naturally fade. Native animal forms and animal forms registered through the framework can each advance when their own growth points meet their usual thresholds. This does not instantly complete any form or affect divine transformations. Choose which parts to show in the mirror.

### Promiscuity

Promiscuity, exhibitionism and deviancy must all reach their **normal ceilings** at the same time. The other two breakthrough traits and increased ceilings are not required.

## Taste for Cruelty

**Unlock**: Enable VanillaPlus and earn the native **Sadomasochist** feat in the **current save**. The native feat requires both sadism and masochism to reach their highest levels, with normal feat eligibility rules. Saves that already earned it immediately display <span class="def">Taste for Cruelty</span>. No extra scene is required.

**Effects**: <span class="def">Your sadism and masochism can stop fading naturally each day</span>. In Attitudes, leave “Reflecting on the pain you have given and received” unchecked to retain them, or check it to resume natural decay. Positive gains, level calculations and other scene effects still follow vanilla. The trait does not directly increase either inclination or restore deliberate reductions from scenes or other effects.

**Settings**: The hypnosis setting does not affect it. Disabling VanillaPlus restores normal decay. Re-enabling it applies the effect according to this save's feat record.

## Iron Will

Once your fallen angel form reaches its stable stage, Iron Will prevents willpower decay. The attitude “Being overwhelmed by pleasure” is checked by default and caps non-combat arousal at **1 below the orgasm threshold**. Uncheck it to allow the normal threshold without losing willpower protection. Combat and Gwylan’s ritual always retain the native threshold.

## Trinity

**Prerequisites**: Enable VanillaPlus and divine transformations in the game settings. Pray in the temple's prayer room and encounter each of the three vanilla presences: warmth, stone and empty darkness. Both embracing a presence and snapping out of it count as experiencing that contact. Your fallen angel form must have **settled into its stable stage**, and you must wear a **dark pendant** when praying.

**Where and when**: Once all three contacts have been recorded, wear a dark pendant during a solar eclipse and use the native **Sneak inside** option at the prayer-room entrance, then pray. This entrance requires the vanilla history level and costs grace. Each prayer has a **10%** chance of a new encounter. The eclipse must still be active when that prayer finishes.

**Choices**: Choose **Embrace** to take a vanilla willpower check. Higher willpower makes it easier to remain conscious, and the option displays the difficulty. Success still causes pain, stress and trauma, increases awareness and grants **Trinity**. Failure brings a stronger shock and reduces control. You can try again during a later eclipse. **Snap out of it** lets you leave, gaining a little stress, awareness and willpower.

**Trait and mirror**: Trinity allows angel, fallen angel and demon forms to coexist and prevents their development points from decaying. Point gain conditions and transformation side effects follow vanilla. Forms you have not gained still need to be developed, and the mirror shows the existing forms' native settings together.

## Eerie mirrors: entry and return

1. Complete the deviancy breakthrough and then reach its new maximum to gain the stage-seven trait. Completing breakthrough story conditions alone is insufficient.
2. Touch the mirror you want to use to discover it. Mirrors include the orphanage, owned homes, farm, tower, temple and character homes. Character homes still need their own access permission.
3. At game time **03:00 until before 04:00**, with tentacles enabled and either hallucinations stage two or the day's mirror-tentacle condition, check for crossing the eerie mirror.
4. Read mirror hints and exits on the plains. This is not a safe fixed teleport lobby. Mirror coordinates are reassigned on every entry.
5. Returning through a different mirror from the entry origin awards **Beyond the Mirror**. When mirrors share nearby coordinates, the actual exit selected matters.
6. Leaving through the entry mirror on the plains or collapsing there **returns you to the mirror you entered through**. Collapsing in labour follows the vanilla mirror-entry route to the hospital.

The other side remains the tentacle plains with vanilla dangers and encounters. Discovering one mirror does not replace another mirror's discovery, home access or the time requirement.

## Hospital services

**Virginity restoration** is offered in the hospital foyer for eligible anatomy, at <span class="gold">£1,000</span> and about one hour per procedure. Body state and chastity equipment restrict eligibility. It restores the relevant physical record without deleting past relationships or story experiences.

## Gargantuan size and multiplayer actions

The PC gains **size 7: Gargantuan**, through existing body-size settings and growth routes. The sidebar has the matching appearance, and NPC size settings also gain a corresponding option. PC size 7 and NPC size 5 use original size-6 images for single-penetration X-ray. Double penetration keeps the vanilla display. Encounter close-ups do not gain dedicated size-7 images. Acceptance and growth notifications still apply.

Growth and fluid recovery continue to follow existing body, drug and transformation rules. Size 7 does not separately grant faster recovery.

After the promiscuity breakthrough, suitable multiplayer encounters offer extra actions; reaching the new ceiling grants its trait. Enough participants, usable body parts and an appropriate position are still required, and native success checks still apply. The trait does not guarantee every action succeeds.
