### Where the love-interest list appears

Open vanilla **Character → Attitudes** and find “Who do you consider to be your love interest?” Click an available character to add them, or a selected character to remove them. Drag selected names or use the arrows to reorder them. The first three also fill the vanilla primary, secondary, and tertiary fields. The selection belongs to the **current save**.

The number of choices follows vanilla **awareness level**. When the list is full, other candidates are disabled; the highest level permits further selections. Candidates are characters the vanilla game currently considers **possible**. Advance their own relationship routes first. This list does not replace their story progression. Moving in together also requires an actual current relationship.

### Small portraits in Social

Open vanilla **Social**. Existing NPC cards gain small portraits and relationship icons. The image follows the NPC's state, and a missing variant falls back to a default expression. These are fan portraits rather than official art. The module does not add a Social card for an NPC who has no vanilla card.

If an icon is absent, check that the NPC has entered Social and that this module and its image assets loaded. The larger portraits shown during scenes belong to the separate **NPCSidebarPortrait** module.
