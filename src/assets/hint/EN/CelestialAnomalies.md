### Enable the events

Enable the solar eclipse and meteor shower separately under **Mod Settings → Celestial Anomalies**.

| Event         | When to watch                                                                    |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Solar eclipse | May occur near the new moon; begins between 07:00 and 08:59 and lasts 9–11 hours |
| Meteor shower | Begins between 20:00 and 22:00 and lasts 5–7 hours, possibly into the next day   |

The sky, weather icon and descriptions change during an event. Dates follow the game calendar.

### Stargazing and music

With Robin Expansion enabled, suggest stargazing in Robin's room or at the beach on a dry meteor-shower night. Robin must be present, free to join you and close enough for a date.

Event music requires Dynamic Music and the separate audio pack.
