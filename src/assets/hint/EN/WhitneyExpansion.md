### Underground brothel rescue

This route opens only while **both the PC and Whitney are held in the underground brothel**. In the **Underground Cell**, choose **“Listen beyond the door (0:01)”** to recognise Whitney's voice, then **“Wait for the guard to open the door (0:30)”**. Once you slip backstage, **“Unfasten the clasp (0:05)”** tests skulduggery, while **“Shove the guard aside (0:05)”** tests physique. If Robin is also captive, Robin distracts a guard and can escape with both of you.

The first escape check starts at **25%**. Skulduggery, physique, and Robin's help can raise it, up to **90%**. The clasp starts at **30%** and the guard charge at **25%**; both rise with the relevant ability and cap at **85%**. Failure returns you to the cell and raises stress, pain, and trauma. You can try again.

Escaping with Whitney grants the rescue achievement. Leaving alone does not count as rescuing Whitney. The module does not force either character into the brothel to begin this route.

### Reunion and daily scenes

After a successful rescue, visit the **school front courtyard on a school day between 07:00 and 18:00** for the reunion. Ask how Whitney is doing; a lover can hold hands. On later days, when Whitney is at school, the PC is not exposed, and no other event occupies the courtyard, speak there once per day. Lovers can walk together. A prefect badge changes Whitney's reaction.

The later school conversations rotate through everyday moments. You need not keep asking about the disappearance.

If reunion is missing, check that the rescue succeeded, Whitney has returned to school, and today is a school day. Daily talk can disappear after it has been used today or while vanilla events occupy Whitney.
