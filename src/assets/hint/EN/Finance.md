## Start here

Enable **Finance** and reload. Enter the financial centre from High Street and select owned-home furniture at the **furniture-shop entrance**. Select the character as a love interest before inviting them to move in.

| Goal                   | Where                                          | Prepare first                                                         |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pay merchants by card  | Financial centre bank counter                  | Account, card, and bank funds or available credit                     |
| Buy property           | Financial centre property counter              | Bank funds, or eligible deposit, fee and capped repayment reserve     |
| Furnish an owned home  | Furniture-shop entrance → owned-home furniture | A purchased usable home, selected before its furniture category       |
| Invite a love interest | Their current vanilla location                 | Love-interest selection, usable bed, capacity, no tenancy or freeze   |
| Check bills            | Journal finance section                        | Accounts, holdings, property totals, payment dates and overdue status |

<span class="teal">Property purchases, loans and recurring bills have their own bank settlement rules. They are not the same as the merchant payment preference.</span>

Check the **journal** for current accounts and tasks, and **statistics** for cumulative deliveries and payments. Find the cheat options under **Cheats → Deadwood Reblooms**.

## Time and accounting periods

When days pass, business and staff accounts settle on their billing dates, including orchard and Robin’s shop investments. Each day’s income comes before debts due that day; vehicle upkeep counts whole weeks.

## Links to street life

With Street Life enabled, the financial centre bank is also the heist target, and a factory packaging line can help with supplies. An incident suspends counter service for two days; existing loans and regular bills still need attention. See **Street Life** for the route and choices.
