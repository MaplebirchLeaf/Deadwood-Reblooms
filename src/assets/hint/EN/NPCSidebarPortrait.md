### See portraits in scenes

NPC Sidebar Portrait displays available artwork for people in the current scene. Outfits, poses and expressions update with the scene.

Small social-card portraits belong to More Love Interests and NPC Avatars. The two features can be enabled separately.

Rain makes clothes damp during confirmed outdoor schedules such as parks, beaches, streets or woodland. Indoor characters do not follow the player’s exposure to rain. Existing scene-specific wetness, such as promenade splashes, takes priority. Tsukihan keeps rain-soaked clothes until bathing and changing.
