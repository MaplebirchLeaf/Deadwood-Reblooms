### Prerequisites and entrance

Enable **LongerCombat**, reload and open **Mod settings → Longer combat**. No character or relationship unlock applies. Continuation depends on the resolution used by the encounter.

### What each option controls

| Option              | Range and default                      | Effect                                                                  |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Seconds per turn    | 5–60, default 10, increments of 5      | Elapsed game time per turn, not recurrence chance                       |
| Maximum rounds      | 1–10, default 3                        | Includes the final resolution, then ends normally                       |
| Recurrence chance   | 5%–100%, default 50%, increments of 5% | Whether an eligible resolution continues, not guaranteed maximum length |
| Infinite encounters | Separate toggle                        | Ignores the round cap while retaining other vanilla exits               |

### Steps

1. Try the defaults in a normal encounter and check its actions and ending.
2. Adjust seconds for elapsed time, chance for recurrence and the cap for bounded length. Disable infinite mode when using a cap.
3. Eligible continuation retains participants and adds phase-specific text and status prompts.
4. Ending early may mean a failed chance roll or reaching the cap. A three-round cap does not guarantee three rounds.
5. Check settings in the next normal encounter rather than repeatedly refreshing combat.

### Ending and excluded resolutions

Humans and beasts use their respective vanilla climax resolutions. The final round executes the story ending. The hawk nest's satisfaction and sleep should also occur at final resolution.

Victory, escape and dedicated story endings are not universally replaced by continuation. Beast resolutions with special arguments remain vanilla. Not every tentacle, plant or special story encounter gains repeated rounds.

### Effects and limits

Changes turn time, eligible continuation and related prose, not skills, opponents or locked stories. Disable infinite mode when you want the cap to end continuation.

### Troubleshooting

Check infinite mode, cap and chance, then distinguish climax, victory and a dedicated ending. Report the entrance, enemy form, last screen, settings and complete error. “Story combat will not end” alone is insufficient to identify the resolution.
