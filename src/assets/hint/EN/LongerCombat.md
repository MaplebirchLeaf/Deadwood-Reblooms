### Consecutive encounter options

Open **Longer Combat** in mod options. Seconds per turn range from **5 to 60**, in steps of 5. The maximum consecutive encounters range from **1 to 10**. Repeat chance ranges from **5% to 100%**, in steps of 5%. Defaults are **10 seconds, 3 encounters, and 50%**. “Unlimited” removes the encounter-count stopping point.

These values affect subsequent combat. Vanilla still handles each NPC's position and basic combat resolution. This module adds stage-dependent dialogue, descriptions of occupied body parts, and fluids after the vanilla resolution. In a group encounter, participants are described separately.

If a climax does not lead to another encounter, check the count limit and remember that the repeat chance is rolled. If a changed option does not appear to apply to the current screen, finish the encounter and check the next one.

### How continuation ends

The round limit includes the final vanilla resolution. The default of three rounds is a maximum, not a guarantee: each climax must also pass the continuation chance. Unlimited mode ignores the round limit and continues attempting another round. Turn it off to allow a natural ending.

Human and beast encounters use their respective vanilla climax routines. Continuation keeps the current opponent; the final climax runs the original story ending. Great Hawk satisfaction and going to sleep remain part of that final resolution. Defeat, escape, and dedicated story resolutions are not forcibly extended. Beast endings with special arguments stay under vanilla control, so some encounters are not extended.

If an encounter never ends, first check unlimited mode. Include the combat entry, finish passage, opponent form, round limit, and chance when reporting a problem.
