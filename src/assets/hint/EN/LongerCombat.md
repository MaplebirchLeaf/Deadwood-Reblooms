### Adjust encounter length

Use **Mod Settings → Longer Combat**:

| Option               | Default and range                | Purpose                                              |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Time per turn        | 10 seconds; adjustable from 5–60 | Sets the game time spent on a turn                   |
| Maximum encounters   | 3; adjustable from 1–10          | Limits the total number of consecutive encounters    |
| Continuation chance  | 50%; adjustable from 5%–100%     | Decides whether an eligible ending continues         |
| Unlimited encounters | Off                              | Ignores both the count limit and continuation chance |

Turn unlimited encounters off to stop at the chosen limit. The count includes the final encounter; a three-encounter limit can still end earlier because of chance.

### Which encounters continue

Encounters using ordinary human or beast climax endings may continue with the same participants. Story aftermath occurs after the final encounter.

Victories, escapes, train encounters and special story endings finish as usual.
