Enable **Sydney** and reload. School, dorm and estate have separate access rules. **Acquaintance, love-interest selection, dorm invitation and estate address** do not substitute for one another.

### Science: unlock and steps

1. Progress the vanilla relationship and attend science normally.
2. The eligible relationship stage opens Sirris's classroom transfer hint.
3. Share lunch with Sydney, ask about transferring and accept.
4. Check later science classes for shared seating and interactions. Attendance still follows schedules.

Notes support learning, while conversation changes love and stress. Some teasing needs Sydney's corruption/lust and the PC's attitude levels. Transfer does not unlock every choice.

<span class="teal">Without the classroom hint, attend science before returning to lunch.</span> Exams, absence and unavailable Sydney are not bypassed by repeatedly visiting lunch.

### Temple dorm: invitation and activities

**Unlock**: obtain monk rank and dorm access, then ask beside Sydney's working area while below maximum stress. The bedside entrance follows acceptance. Other temple ranks do not automatically replace this first-invitation requirement.

| Activity                   | Steps                                               | Effects and limits                                                                        |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Reading                    | Select reading, about ten minutes                   | History-related dialogue and limited daily benefits, with ability-dependent extra content |
| Conversation               | Choose the day's topic while present                | Relationship activity with daily limits                                                   |
| Wardrobe and bed           | Use dorm storage and clothing/bed options           | Access and presence remain checked, without summoning Sydney                              |
| Transformation interaction | Sufficient love and a visible completed animal part | Daily limits, with no option for hidden or incomplete parts                               |
| Sleeping and night waking  | Choose sleep duration at the accessible bunk        | Reads presence, rest and current events rather than occurring every night                 |

Romantic intimacy also requires Sydney selected in Attitudes. Duties, punishment and schedules take priority. Invitation does not guarantee access to Sydney all day.

### Chastity: requirements and outcomes

After the vanilla corrupt-room story, ask at the temple while Sydney is a love interest, not wearing a belt, no evaluation is pending and the PC has not promised to Sydney.

- Pure Sydney may voluntarily accompany you to Jordan.
- Corrupt Sydney refuses, while wavering Sydney has different responses.
- Refusal does not secretly change belt state or become agreement through repeated clicks.

### Festivals, effects and troubleshooting

Halloween and Christmas follow vanilla dates and events. The cross-character Halloween achievement requires relevant visits in the same year, not one arbitrary estate visit.

Adds school, dorm and family life without cancelling temple duties, imprisonment, festivals or punishment. Check **specific permission → location → vanilla stage → selection → time, stress and exposure → daily limits**.
