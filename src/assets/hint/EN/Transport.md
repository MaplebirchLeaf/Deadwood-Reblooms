You can own one vehicle of each model. Select Sell on an owned model in the showroom when it is parked there. The journal records parking, condition, fuel and arrears.
Bicycles start at **£1,500**. Buy them in the <span class="blue">shopping centre</span>, and motorcycles and cars on <span class="blue">Harvest Street</span>. Everyday runabouts and luxury sports cars differ in price, journey times and suitable terrain. Registering a motor vehicle requires an <span class="teal">ID</span>.

## Learning to drive

Take your ID to the driving school and apply for a <span class="teal">provisional licence</span>. Collect it the next day. Cars and motorcycles have separate qualifications, with theory and practical driving taught separately. You can take further revision sessions and driving lessons while the school is open. Practice improves driving proficiency.

- **Cars:** pass the theory test, then prepare for the road test. Its four sections ask you to observe the road and choose suitable actions. Minor faults are recorded. <span class="red">A dangerous fault fails the test.</span>
- **Motorcycles:** complete six hours of basic training, then prepare for the theory, manoeuvres and road tests. The certificate permits practice on a small scooter. Riding requires a helmet.
- **At the test centre:** a borderline result caused by minor faults may leave an opportunity to bluff or bribe the examiner. Being caught brings police attention and suspends bookings for **three days**.

Theory passes, basic training certificates and full driving qualifications are <span class="green">valid for life</span>. After a failed test, you can book again the next day. The school closes on Sundays. Snow and heavy rain suspend driving lessons and practical tests.

Driving proficiency appears in your <span class="blue">characteristics</span>. Better handling, braking and awareness can shorten road journeys by up to **30%**, or trail journeys by up to **15%**. Rain and snow still slow you down.

## Parking and maintenance

Vehicles stay where you last parked them. Return to the parking spot to use them, or pay the garage for recovery. Home garages have separate capacities for bicycles, motorcycles and motor vehicles.

Visit the petrol station for fuel and the garage for repairs. Repair costs depend on the vehicle's value and damage, making luxury vehicles more expensive to maintain. Each model has its own wear rate. Road travel wears condition by **0.28%–0.55% per hour** for bicycles, **0.22%–0.38%** for motorcycles and **0.14%–0.34%** for cars. Unpaved trails usually cause **1.5 times** the wear, reduced to **1.15 times** for a mountain bicycle or **1.2 times** for a trail motorcycle. The restored lake road uses the road rate. <span class="red">A vehicle at zero condition needs repairs before it can be driven.</span> Showroom buybacks start at half the purchase price and fall with condition.

Each residential garage holds **four 20-litre petrol cans**. Choose a delivery address at the petrol station to buy a full can or refill stored cans. Delivery costs **£5** and takes **30 minutes**. A new can costs another **£20**. Fuel costs **£1.70 per litre**, the same as the pumps.

Park a vehicle inside that garage to refuel from a can. This takes **5 minutes** and transfers only what fits in the tank. Spare fuel remains in storage, and empty cans can be refilled. Each property has its own fuel stock.

The “Available vehicles” section appears above the street map and lists each vehicle parked nearby. Select a model to choose a destination. Roads and accessible wilderness paths also let you select a vehicle directly. Move stored vehicles out of the garage before departing.

Each vehicle incurs upkeep every seven days from its purchase, paid directly from your bank account. An unpaid balance becomes arrears, which must be settled before driving or selling. Fuel is consumed separately for each journey.

Bicycle shops, motorcycle showrooms and workshops sell repair kits. Tools can be reused and come with three sets of consumable supplies. A roadside repair takes **15 minutes** for a bicycle or **30 minutes** for a motor vehicle. Housekeeping skill improves the repair, up to **60%** condition. Supplies can be restocked.

Fuel and condition appear in the <span class="blue">vehicle menu header</span>. Check both before a long journey.

The bars shorten as their values fall: <span class="green">green</span> at **75%** or more, <span class="teal">teal</span> at **50%**, <span class="lblue">light blue</span> at **25%**, and <span class="red">red</span> below that.

## Roads and trails

Travel between the town streets and farmlands. The menu lists streets and the farmlands as destinations, while the farm road sections determine distance. Athletics helps shorten bicycle journeys, and cycling grants a little athletics experience.

- **Park:** bicycles can enter. Motor vehicles stay on nearby streets.
- **Forest:** bicycles can reach the outskirts. Mountain bikes can follow the trail in short stages until dense woodland forces you onto foot. Restoring the road towards the lake lets other bicycles use that section too.
- **Moor:** mountain bikes and trail motorcycles can follow tracks near the edge in dry weather. Wet ground, rocks and deeper terrain require walking.
- **Narrow alleyways, tunnels and bogs:** leave your vehicle near the entrance and continue on foot.

Trail journeys still advance exploration and hunts. Forest trails can lead to harassment, while the restored lake road follows vanilla safety rules. After a quiet ride, continue from your current depth or dismount to look around. Remember where you parked before leaving the road.

## By the roadside

Road details and passenger conversations vary between journeys. In dry daytime weather, ordinary road journeys lasting at least **five minutes** have a **25%** chance of a roadside encounter, at most once a day. Someone may need help contacting a garage or gathering spilled shopping. You can help or continue on your way.

Gathering shopping takes **two minutes**, and arranging repairs takes **five**. Helping reduces stress a little. With **300 housekeeping**, a motor repair kit and supplies, you can also fix a loose connector, using one supply. School and temple lifts take your passenger straight to the agreed destination.

## Companions and homes

Depart from a garage or street to reach bus-stop streets and accessible owned homes. When leaving a garage, invite cohabiting partners who are currently at home. Passenger capacity depends on the seats. A convertible ride grants extra affection once per partner each day. Cliff, Starfish, Danube and High Streets offer a short outing together, rewarded once per partner each day.

On school days, offer to drive when Robin, Whitney or Sydney is ready to walk to school with you. After lessons, offer a lift home when you meet Robin or Whitney by the school gate, or Kylar in the rear courtyard; Kylar's home must already be unlocked. When Sydney is leaving the library or rehearsal for the temple, you can offer a lift there. Your car must be parked nearby, with a passenger seat and enough fuel. Each type of lift grants affection once per partner each day.

When you meet Alex shopping in town, you can offer a lift back towards the farm. Your car must be parked on that street, with a spare seat and enough fuel for the country road. You need not be dating Alex; completing the lift increases affection once per day. The car stays beside the road in the farmlands; entering the farm still uses the original track.

When Avery arrives to pick you up after school, you can mention that you brought your own vehicle. Avery's response varies with anger and your position in the company. If Robin is there too, you can invite Robin to ride home to the orphanage.

Exposure, bound arms or shackles prevent departure. Drinking, drugs or maximum stress prevent driving motor vehicles.
