## Getting started

Choose a vehicle for the work you want to do. Bicycles suit short trips; motorcycles need a helmet and the corresponding qualification. Cars carry passengers, and some models carry cargo or work as taxis. Begin motor-vehicle training at the Elk Street driving school. After buying, remember where you parked and choose destinations from the vehicle's street menu.

## Buying and using vehicles

You can own one vehicle of each model. Select Sell on an owned model in the showroom when it is parked there. The journal records parking, condition, fuel and arrears.
Bicycles start at **<span class="gold">£1,500</span>**. Buy them in the <span class="blue">shopping centre</span>, and motorcycles and cars on <span class="blue">Harvest Street</span>. Everyday runabouts and luxury sports cars differ in price, journey times and suitable terrain. Registering a motor vehicle requires an <span class="teal">ID</span>.

## Learning to drive

Take your ID to the driving school and apply for a <span class="teal">provisional licence</span>. Collect it the next day. Cars and motorcycles have separate qualifications, with theory and practical driving taught separately. You can take further revision sessions and driving lessons while the school is open. Practice improves driving proficiency.

- **Cars:** pass the theory test, then prepare for the road test. Its four sections ask you to observe the road and choose suitable actions. Minor faults are recorded. <span class="red">A dangerous fault fails the test.</span>
- **Motorcycles:** complete six hours of basic training, then prepare for the theory, manoeuvres and road tests. The certificate permits practice on a small scooter. Riding requires a helmet.
- **At the test centre:** a borderline result caused by minor faults may leave an opportunity to bluff or bribe the examiner. Being caught brings police attention and suspends bookings for **three days**.

Theory passes, basic training certificates and full driving qualifications are <span class="green">valid for life</span>. After a failed test, you can book again the next day. The school closes on Sundays. Snow and heavy rain suspend driving lessons and practical tests.

Driving proficiency appears in your <span class="blue">characteristics</span>. Better handling, braking and awareness can shorten road journeys by up to **30%**, or trail journeys by up to **15%**. Rain and snow still slow you down.

## Parking and maintenance

Vehicles stay where you last parked them. Return to the parking spot to use them, or pay the garage for recovery. Home garages have separate capacities for bicycles, motorcycles and motor vehicles.

Visit the petrol station for fuel and the garage for repairs. Repair costs depend on the vehicle's value and damage, making luxury vehicles more expensive to maintain. Each model has its own wear rate. Road travel wears condition by **0.28%–0.55% per hour** for bicycles, **0.22%–0.38%** for motorcycles and **0.14%–0.34%** for cars. Unpaved trails usually cause **1.5 times** the wear, reduced to **1.15 times** for a mountain bicycle or **1.2 times** for a trail motorcycle. The restored lake road uses the road rate. <span class="red">A vehicle at zero condition needs repairs before it can be driven.</span> Showroom buybacks start at half the purchase price and fall with condition.

Each residential garage holds **four 20-litre petrol cans**. Choose a delivery address at the petrol station to buy a full can or refill stored cans. Delivery costs **<span class="gold">£5</span>** and takes **30 minutes**. A new can costs another **<span class="gold">£20</span>**. Fuel costs **<span class="gold">£1.70</span> per litre**, the same as the pumps.

Park a vehicle inside that garage to refuel from a can. This takes **5 minutes** and transfers only what fits in the tank. Spare fuel remains in storage, and empty cans can be refilled. Each property has its own fuel stock.

The “Available vehicles” section appears above the street map and lists each vehicle parked nearby. Select a model to choose a destination. Roads and accessible wilderness paths also let you select a vehicle directly. Move stored vehicles out of the garage before departing.

Each vehicle incurs upkeep every seven days from its purchase, paid automatically from your bank balance if you have an account. An insufficient balance or no account leaves arrears. Pay cash from the vehicle menu or sale page, or pay from an open bank account without needing a debit card. Clear the arrears before driving or selling. Fuel is consumed separately for each journey.

Jumping forward also counts any upkeep weeks that have passed. Settling the same date again does not charge twice, and moving backwards does not refund upkeep already paid.

Bicycle shops, motorcycle showrooms and workshops sell repair kits. Tools can be reused and come with three sets of consumable supplies. A roadside repair takes **15 minutes** for a bicycle or **30 minutes** for a motor vehicle. Housekeeping skill improves the repair, up to **60%** condition. Supplies can be restocked.

Fuel and condition appear in the <span class="blue">vehicle menu header</span>. Check both before a long journey.

The bars shorten as their values fall: <span class="green">green</span> at **75%** or more, <span class="teal">teal</span> at **50%**, <span class="lblue">light blue</span> at **25%**, and <span class="red">red</span> below that.

## Roads and trails

Travel between the town streets and farmlands. The menu lists streets and the farmlands as destinations, while the farm road sections determine distance. Athletics helps shorten bicycle journeys, and cycling grants a little athletics experience.

- **Park:** bicycles can enter. Motor vehicles stay on nearby streets.
- **Forest:** bicycles can reach the outskirts. Mountain bikes can follow the trail in short stages until dense woodland forces you onto foot. Restoring the road towards the lake lets other bicycles use that section too.
- **Moor:** mountain bikes and trail motorcycles can follow tracks near the edge in dry weather. Wet ground, rocks and deeper terrain require walking.
- **Narrow alleyways, tunnels and bogs:** leave your vehicle near the entrance and continue on foot.

Trail journeys still advance exploration and hunts. Forest trails can lead to harassment, while the restored lake road follows vanilla safety rules. After a quiet ride, continue from your current depth or dismount to look around. Remember where you parked before leaving the road.

## Roadside events

Ordinary road journeys lasting at least **5 minutes** can meet a roadblock, aggressive overtaking, spilled cargo or a punctured tyre. Daytime trips can also encounter someone needing help. The encounter and its roll are fixed on departure, with at most one encounter per day. Night, rain, snow and poor condition affect the chance or type of encounter. School and temple lifts still follow their agreed route directly.

Encounters pause the journey partway through. Time, fuel and wear already spent remain spent. You can give way or attempt a risky manoeuvre. <span class="teal">Driving, athletics, physique, housekeeping and skulduggery</span> help with judging gaps, handling bicycles, clearing obstacles, repairing tyres and spotting a trap. Stress, fatigue, condition, chassis and weather affect the displayed chance of making a gap. Physique requirements scale with your current body capacity.

- **Roadblocks**: turning back and taking another road costs **10 minutes**. At **400 skulduggery**, you can read the ambush and slip past. You can offer **<span class="gold">£50</span> in cash** or attempt the gap. <span class="red">Failing to clear the gap starts a fight.</span> You can also fight back directly, using the original combat actions. Defeat the attackers or attract help to keep your vehicle and continue if you are fit to travel. If you are too hurt to resist, stop resisting or surrender the vehicle, it is stolen.
- **Dangerous overtaking and spilled cargo**: giving way takes **4 minutes**; waiting for crates to be cleared takes **8 minutes**. Strong characters can help shift the crates. Failed risky manoeuvres damage the vehicle.
- **Punctures**: **300 housekeeping**, the appropriate tools and one repair supply allow a repair. A passing driver can contact the workshop: **<span class="gold">£20</span>** for a bicycle or **<span class="gold">£50</span>** for a motor vehicle, with a **30-minute** wait. Limping onwards adds **15 minutes** and costs **6%** condition. You can also leave the vehicle and continue on foot.
- **Unwanted touching**: a taxi passenger may reach into the front seat during a fare, with drunk passengers more likely to do so. Willpower, English and current stress affect a firm warning; sufficient physique lets you stop and force the passenger out. A failed warning or active resistance starts an original combat encounter with the same passenger who boarded. Ejecting the passenger or ending the encounter cancels the fare and leaves the car at the incident location. An unfinished journey earns no fare.

Stolen private vehicles are marked in the journal and can be traced at the workshop. This adds **<span class="gold">£50</span>** to the normal collection charge, takes **3 hours**, and leaves the recovered vehicle with **8%** less condition. Report a stolen company car to dispatch; the company handles the search and pays completed bookings. Stopping with a passenger cancels the unfinished booking without paying its fare.

Picking up spilled shopping takes **2 minutes**; arranging workshop help takes **5 minutes** and slightly reduces stress. At **300 housekeeping**, motor repair tools and supplies also let you fix a loose connector on another driver's car.

## Companions and homes

Depart from a garage or street to reach bus-stop streets and accessible owned homes. When leaving a garage, invite cohabiting partners who are currently at home. Passenger capacity depends on the seats. A convertible ride grants extra affection once per partner each day. Cliff, Starfish, Danube and High Streets offer a short outing together, rewarded once per partner each day.

On school days, offer to drive when Robin, Whitney or Sydney is ready to walk to school with you. After lessons, offer a lift home when you meet Robin or Whitney by the school gate, or Kylar in the rear courtyard; Kylar's home must already be unlocked. When Sydney is leaving the library or rehearsal for the temple, you can offer a lift there. Your car must be parked nearby, with a passenger seat and enough fuel. Each type of lift grants affection once per partner each day.

When you meet Alex shopping in town, you can offer a lift back towards the farm. Your car must be parked on that street, with a spare seat and enough fuel for the country road. You need not be dating Alex; completing the lift increases affection once per day. The car stays beside the road in the farmlands; entering the farm still uses the original track.

When Avery arrives to pick you up after school, you can mention that you brought your own vehicle. Avery's response varies with anger and your position in the company. If Robin is there too, you can invite Robin to ride home to the orphanage.

You normally need suitable clothing to depart. With Vanilla Enhancement’s exhibitionism ability and comfort at your current exposure level, you may keep your appearance while offering a ride. Taking back school clothes is your choice; rides near the school also offer “Retrieve clothes, then offer the ride”. Departing does not dress you automatically. Bound arms or restrictive shackles still prevent departure; alcohol, drugs and maximum stress still prevent driving motor vehicles.

## Taxi work

Register at the <span class="blue">taxi desk in the Harvest Street dealership</span>. Bring your ID and full car licence. Registration and a radio cost **<span class="gold">£75</span>**, take **15 minutes**, and remain valid. The desk opens **08:00–18:00**. Once on duty, the radio continues dispatching evening bookings.

- **Company car:** collect a fully fuelled four-door saloon. The company covers petrol and upkeep. You receive **55%** of fares and all tips when you return the keys. The working car has its own record.
- **Your own car:** use a used, four-door or long-wheelbase saloon with at least **50%** condition and paid upkeep. Receive **80%** of fares and all tips after each journey, while paying your own fuel and maintenance costs. Registered drivers can also start work from their saloon's street menu. <span class="teal">Long-wheelbase saloons can take executive bookings at a 35% higher tariff.</span>

Waiting for dispatch takes **5 minutes** and offers up to three bookings, each valid for **45 minutes**. Commuters, shoppers, hospital passengers, seaside visitors and evening customers appear at suitable hours. Passenger and luggage capacity affect the bookings. Drive empty to the pickup point, then deliver the passenger to the agreed street. Both legs consume fuel and condition. Check that the tank covers both before accepting.

Fares start at **<span class="gold">£8</span>** and rise with road distance. The **20:00–06:00** night tariff is **25%** higher. Driving proficiency shortens the journey; at **600**, an uneventful, on-time journey adds **<span class="gold">£1</span>** to the tip. Helping with luggage, taking bends gently or chatting at the lights also affects tips. Housekeeping improves luggage assistance. Gentle driving adds two minutes. If a passenger offers less than agreed, accept it or spend three minutes pursuing payment. At **300** skulduggery, you recognise the excuse and recover the full fare.

Refuse a passenger before departure or notify dispatch to cancel an interrupted journey. Completed bookings add a little fatigue and stress. Business fame increases up to five times a day. The journal records completed bookings, total pay received and uncollected company-car wages.

Own-car drivers can sign off at the final street. Return a company car to Harvest Street to finish the shift. If you cannot keep driving, ask the company to collect it: wait **20 minutes**, with up to **<span class="gold">£30</span>** taken from shift earnings. Complete or cancel the current booking before signing off.
