# Welcome to Deadwood Reblooms

> Some branches flowered once, only to be left behind by a long season. When spring returns, even quiet rings can send out new shoots.

Deadwood Reblooms gathers branches from paths that never quite reached their end: some once flourished, some stopped midway, and some survived only as an idea still worth remembering. Rooted once more in familiar soil, they unfold into new forms along the streets, characters, and rules you already know.

The town remains familiar, but old branches are budding again. Some changes are visible the moment you look up; others reveal themselves only after many ordinary days.

## Look up at the sky

A solar eclipse is more than sudden darkness. It has a predictable time, a complete sequence of stages, and light that changes across the town. When night falls, meteor showers may cross the clouds and leave their mark both in the sky and on the sidebar weather display.

These events follow their own schedules instead of being rerolled whenever a save is loaded. You may wait for them, miss them, or plan ahead for the night they arrive.

## Old branches flower again

- **Vanilla enhancements** let willpower, physique, deviancy, exhibitionism, beauty, and promiscuity continue beyond their old limits, with breakthroughs grounded in the original world.
- **People and relationships** add new moments with familiar characters and allow love interests to grow beyond three abruptly limited places.
- **Social sidebar portraits** change with a character's relationships, moods, and experiences. They are not a row of static identity photos, but expressions left behind by your shared story.
- **More transformations** connect new body parts, traits, clothing interactions, and everyday details back into the original systems instead of adding only another status icon.

## The body remembers

Longer encounters give situations time to develop, while fluid traces on NPCs remain visible on their models. You can also create custom bodywriting, collect and invoke your own incantation-like commands, and decide whether Bailey's unpaid rent should carry into the following week.

None of these systems must be enabled as one inseparable package. They are separate branches; keep only those that belong in your story.

## Let it grow your way

`DR` is the root module for Deadwood Reblooms. Disabling it disables the entire mod. While the root remains enabled, the Maplebirch Framework module manager still lets you individually enable or disable celestial anomalies, vanilla enhancements, more transformations, relationship features, and other modules.

Before your first playthrough, it is worth doing three things:

1. Open the module manager and review which branches are currently enabled.
2. Reload the game when the framework asks, so every module state takes full effect.
3. Keep a save from before enabling the mod, making it easy to try different combinations.
